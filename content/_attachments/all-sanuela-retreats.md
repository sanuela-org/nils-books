# Deep Presence & Heart Connection Retreat

6 Day Deep Presence & Heart Connection Retreat Tenerife

## Introduction

This is not a group retreat, but a **private, one-on-one retreat** for those who have been through a lot and want a fresh start. Perhaps you have recently experienced a breakup or divorce, years of emotional distance or numbness, or simply a quiet loneliness that comfort, busyness and distractions could never quite fill. There comes a time when you simply don't want to live like that anymore.

This is a personal, six-day (flexible) full-immersion solo retreat in Tenerife, designed to help you truly open up. Through honest sharing, deep inner work, forgiveness practices, heart-centred somatic experiences, and (if you wish) raw food cleansing or fasting, you can gradually release old protective layers.

**You can explore and practice** how to communicate with honesty and ease about your fears and insecurities, your longings and allowances, your boundaries and your shame. You can discover new, warmer and more authentic ways of connecting with others than most of us learned.

**After the retreat,** you will find that communicating with vulnerability and honesty, offering a deep energetic presence, and connecting with others in an authentic and caring way will come more naturally to you. You will probably also start to attract more souls into your life who feel and live the same way.

This is about opening up again after perhaps a long period of inner withdrawal, and becoming a fully loving and fully lovable human being with an open, curious and adventurous heart once again.

## What Makes This Retreat Special

Your nervous system needs to feel safe enough to open. **This solo retreat meets you where you are.**

Honest sharing, emotional work, shadow work, the practice of forgiveness and gratitude will meet this openness and begin to release what has been holding you back. If you like, you can combine this with fasting or raw food cleansing. It quiets the energetic noise and creates a deeper inner receptivity.

Woven throughout the experience is the lived practice of deep, heart-centred presence and connection. Saying what you actually want and need, clearly and kindly. Learning to offer your hand. Staying present in a long hug, using it to open your heart further and further.

This is not a group workshop or spiritual performance, and it is not about theoretical teaching. At Finca Sanuela, you can experience this heart opening for yourself in a small, authentic setting, either one-to-one or with one or two other guests. Nils will guide you personally at whatever pace you are comfortable with.

## The Retreat Experience

**It doesn't matter what time you arrive.** After picking you up from the nearby bus station, you can take some time to settle in. We can talk, share and get to know each other a bit. If you like, we can draw some oracle cards together and receive intuitive messages about your retreat experience.\
\
When you feel ready, I will show you how heart-centred breathing works. As this forms the basis of your retreat experience, I will remind you regularly to help you maintain the practice, because it is simple to do but difficult to keep going with.

We can then sit together for the first honest sharing of the retreat. This is not about what you want to achieve, but about what you are actually carrying with you. What has been weighing you down? What have you been avoiding? What has your heart been trying to say?

This is the first practice of something you will come back to again and again over the coming days: speaking openly from your heart, without performing, without editing yourself into something more acceptable.

From here, the retreat finds its own rhythm. Mornings often begin quietly, with a walk in nature or time to write or simply be. There is no fixed programme. What arises each day guides what we do with it.

Notice what changes when your body shifts to higher frequencies: how your emotions and your energetic perceptions become more accessible, how your inner voice grows clearer and how something that felt numb begins to stir.

As the retreat unfolds, we move step by step into deeper levels of exploration. This is an invitation to engage with the parts of yourself that have been hiding: the fears, the shame, the unforgiven moments, the old stories about what LOVE is supposed to look like. This happens naturally during our daily activities: during a nature walk, after a shared meal or simply while sitting together.

You will become more confident by practising the simple acts of honest closeness that most of us were never taught. How to say what you want and need with warmth and clarity. How to offer your hand during a walk and feel that as natural. If you feel like it, you can also experience the deeply healing and nourishing effects of a hugging meditation, and learn how to share this experience with others.

By the final day, something has usually shifted. The heart is a little less guarded. Words are spoken more honestly. The longing for authentic connection feels less like a wound and more like a compass. This is where your next chapter begins.

## About the Accomodation

The darkroom space is located in an old bodega (wine cellar) at the back of a natural garden, far from any neighbours in the Tenerife countryside. The space is simple, with plastered lime walls to ensure good air quality. There are no decorations, as these are not required for your sacred ceremony.

The spacious main room contains a double bed and plenty of extra space for putting your things down, doing stretches or yoga, walking around, and eating and drinking.

The meditation cave contains a mattress and a chair, and you can also write in your diary here. If you are sensitive to subtle energies, you may wish to spend most of your time here, as many people feel Gaia's (Mother Earth's) vibrations directly infusing the room.

There is also an additional bathroom cave with a shower, sink, and toilet.

## Full Program

Before confirming your booking for a dark retreat, we will have a brief conversation to get to know each other and answer any questions you may have. There is also a PDF available to help you prepare for this experience.

It's best not to have too many specific ideas or plans, so that you can follow your own flow and direction when your dark room ceremony begins. You can start the retreat at a time that suits you, and we'll arrange the timings together.

Once you have familiarised yourself with your surroundings, you will probably find it easy to navigate. You will soon realise that time no longer exists inside the darkness. It's just you in the eternal Now! You can use this wonderful space to explore yourself.

There is much to discover simply by gazing into the depths of the darkness. Although no light enters the space, it never looks completely black; there are patterns, perhaps dots and shapes. Look long enough and they may start to become entry channels into higher dimensions, revealing ever-changing visions.

When you've had enough of watching, you might feel like doing some yoga stretches. Or some breathwork? Many people experience a range of emotions. It is beautiful to observe how sadness can feel liberating and transform into something more open, eventually becoming pure joy. Now is the time to do so, and you will feel lighter and more centred afterwards.

There are many different paths you can explore. Trust your intuition and inner guidance in this space. During our integration talks in the morning, you will be able to identify areas where you can delve deeper or explore other possibilities. A lot can happen in your inner universe in 24 hours. It often feels good to share and let go before moving on to your next inner processes.
