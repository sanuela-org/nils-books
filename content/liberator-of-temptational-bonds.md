# Liberator of Temptational Bonds

Nils Klippstein's spiritual fantasy about Alistair Rosevale, the liberator who frees Sanuela's souls from their thorns of craving. The story carries the book's own medicine: Alistair grows up inside a family of addictions, discovers the astral beings behind craving, and finds the path out — self-LOVE, fasting and the willingness to return to the underworld to guide others. Four guided meditations and the appendix carry the practice into the reader's life.

## Summary

Cravings have keepers: the thorns of craving, low vibrational astral entities that hook into the astral body, feed on a soul's cravings and defend the ego that shelters them. Alistair Rosevale grows up between his father's wine and his mother's euphoric [[glossary#Euphoric Crystalglaze|crystalglaze]], inherits both, and finds an ancient scripture that names what no one around him can see.

In a mountain enclave near Niramir, High Priestess Calantha guides him through [[hugging-meditations|hugging meditations]] and a fast that starves the thorns and raises his frequencies. Back in Altaris he builds a sanctuary for craving souls — and later walks the path backwards, a documented return to crystalglaze, to understand the people he serves. His deepest finding: behind every craving stands a lack of self-LOVE, and no substance can fill it.

Four [[guided-meditations-for-cravings|guided meditations]] carry the path to the reader, and the appendix dares to imagine a world without artificially concentrated substances.

## Key ideas

- Cravings have keepers: astral thorns hook into the astral body and feed on the cravings they provoke.
- Behind every craving stands a lack of self-LOVE; food can occupy the body but never fill the emotional void.
- Fasting starves the thorns and raises the astral frequencies; the body learns to draw energy directly from the astral realms.
- Days of full indulgence, planned and regret-free, become a contrast exercise that makes fasting easier to love.
- The willing return to addiction teaches a liberator compassion for the souls still asleep in it.
- Clean nourishment is a whole-body matter: gut bacteria, alkaline balance and whole foods decide which cravings survive.
