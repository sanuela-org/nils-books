# Ch 7 — Who Is 'The Universe'?

## What this chapter explores

New Age spiritual language often addresses guidance, testing and delivery to the universe. The phrase can turn a personal relationship into a transaction with a cosmic vending machine or a plea to a celestial lottery. Beyond it sits a [[personal-universe|personal universe]], and [[gaia|Gaia]], who provides our incarnated experience.

## The message

Quantum physics and ancient Eastern wisdom say we each live in a subjective personal universe with its own rules and [[magick|magick]]. We are holographic fragments of a larger consciousness we cannot directly perceive. Re-speaking the familiar lines about "the universe" as "my personal universe" is a magickal act reclaiming creative authority.

We are truly in relationship with Gaia, who provides our incarnated experience. She is a living, loving, highly evolved conscious being, rightly called a goddess by earth-honouring cultures. The water on her surface mirrors the same percentage that we have in our bodies, a reminder that we are part of her.

Gaia lends us bodies made of her elements and everything we need to grow. The energetic and spiritual fabric of reality, our perception of it, the laws of nature and the cycles of life are her creation to educate and evolve the souls within it. 'The universe provides' means Gaia provides: she is the local, conscious administrator of [[intention-and-divine-will|divine will]].

God/Source/All That Is is the infinite totality; Gaia is an individualised consciousness who became a creator herself, designing a planetary school and caring for us as her children. For earthly matters, turn to our Mother first. She is here. She is listening. She understands the experience she designed.

Working magick on Earth means collaborating consciously with Gaia. Asking her support does not diminish God; it honours the beautiful planetary system God created. It asks no one to abandon a belief in God or a saint, only to expand it. 'Gaia' translates as 'the divine presence in our world'.
