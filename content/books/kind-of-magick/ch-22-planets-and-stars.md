# Ch 22 — Planets and Stars

## What this chapter explores

Planets and stars are superconscious beings to relate to and work with. Our ancestors honoured them not out of superstition, but as powerful gods and goddesses who help shape destiny and influence life on Earth. The Moon's phases and the seven-day week offer rhythms for practice.

## In essence

Once [[gaia|Gaia]] is recognised as a goddess, what about the other celestial beings in our sky? Stars or planets are not devoid of consciousness, spirit, influence or life in a different form. They are superconscious beings whose existence and purpose far surpass human understanding. Through the lens of [[magick|magick]], the cosmos is a vast, interconnected family of intelligent gods and goddesses.

Working with planetary energies means entering a conscious relationship with these celestial deities, a loving collaboration between the microcosm and the macrocosm. Planets do not give you energy from outside; they mirror and amplify qualities already inside you.

Each celestial neighbour is described with qualities, uses, symbols, shadow sides, an affirmation and a growth path. The Moon serves as a cosmic mirror, reflecting our hidden, subconscious aspects back to us. Practice can be synchronised with its phases: the New Moon for planting seeds and new beginnings, the Full Moon for charging, revealing truth, gratitude and releasing what is complete.

The seven-day week helps us remember and connect with the distinct frequencies of each planetary consciousness. Yet your focused intention is always the strongest driving force: a conscious decision takes precedence over the calendar.
