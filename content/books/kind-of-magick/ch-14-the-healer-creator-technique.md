# Ch 14 — The Healer & Creator Technique

## What this chapter explores

The [[healer-creator-technique|Healer & Creator technique]] combines energetic healing and [[magick|magick]], bridging intention and embodiment. A simple, flowing movement gathers personal life force (chi) from the earth up through the body into the expanded energy field. Once the energy flows, you learn to shape it: cleansing yourself and your surroundings, infusing yourself with qualities such as courage or LOVE, supporting others.

## In essence

The Healer & Creator Energy Flow exercise is a slow breathing-and-movement circuit, first mentioned in the author's 2019 book *You Are Healer and Creator* and its instruction videos. The H&C Energy Sphere exercise then is about feeling, forming and sending a sphere of life energy.

There are more specific H&C applications available. They all follow the same procedure: about ten passes of the flow exercise, then sending out energetic spheres. The first application turns inward: self-healing and harmonisation. In this variation, the drawn-up energy trickles down as a shower, and the spheres are directed onto your own body. Five minutes a day between existing morning and afternoon habits makes the practice a part of daily life.

It is also possible to embody specific energetic qualities from any [[archetypes|archetype]], an [[the-five-elements|element]], an archangel or a crystal into the flow. The Mother brings nurturing LOVE, Fire brings transformation and courage, Archangel Michael brings protection, rose quartz brings unconditional LOVE. The infused spheres are sent onward: into your surroundings, towards a goal or, [[magick-and-consent|with consent]], to another person.

Further applications — expanding your energetic field, cleansing and nourishing your space, charging a talisman, healing [[gaia|Gaia]], cultivating healthy eating, [[shadow-work|embracing your shadow sides]] — are suggestions to inspire your intuition.
