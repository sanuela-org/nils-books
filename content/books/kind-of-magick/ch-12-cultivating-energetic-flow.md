# Ch 12 — Cultivating Energetic Flow

## What this chapter explores

A highly vibrational state that aligns with [[magick|magick]] is not achieved simply by a couple of meditation sessions; it must be cultivated every single day. The terrain here is energy and its fundamental property, movement, together with the daily practices that move energy directly: walking, breathing, humming, touch.

## The message

Energy's fundamental property is movement: stuck or frozen energy cannot create anything, and the observing consciousness needs energy and movement to create. Some take the masculine path of willpower and discipline; others follow the great creatrix within, the intuition that knows what will serve us best. The best way to raise your vibration is simply to follow it. 'Intuitive hours', free from judgement or agenda, chosen with full presence, let the vibration rise as well.

- Deliberate vigorous [[cultivating-energetic-flow|walking]] — ideally uphill, not a leisurely stroll but proper exercise — awakens the life force, a raw clean charge ready to be focused with intention and infused with LOVE.
- [[heart-centred-breathing#The practice|Heart-Centred Breathing]] draws LOVE into the heart centre and sends it through body and space, providing grounding and protection from egocentric intentions.
- A [[cultivating-energetic-flow|low, steady hum]] stimulates the vagus nerve; before a ritual it gathers power, during a practice it maintains the state.
- In [[cultivating-energetic-flow|silent handholding]], partners breathe LOVE through the heart, letting boundaries blur until energies merge into a shared field.
- [[hugging-meditations#The Practice|Hugging meditations]] bring chests, bellies and hips into contact while partners breathe together and imagine auras merging into one luminous bubble that dissolves separation and balances energies.
