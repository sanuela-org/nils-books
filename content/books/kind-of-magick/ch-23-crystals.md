# Ch 23 — Crystals

## What this chapter explores

Crystals and stones serve as [[magickal-companions|magickal companions]], each with its own frequency and energetic signature. They amplify and anchor intentions and stabilise and ground the raised energies of powerful rituals and practices.

## In essence

Within [[magick|magick]], you do not just use crystals for their supposed properties. You invite them to partner with you, amplifying and anchoring your intentions with their stable, crystalline energy. Each stone holds a unique frequency and energetic signature forged deep within [[gaia|Gaia]], and a crystal's precise structure holds a vibrational pattern with exceptional clarity. A rose quartz within the ritual space is not just a symbol of LOVE; it becomes a concentrated source of heart-centred LOVE vibrations, amplifying that quality throughout your practice.

Crystals also stabilise and ground energies. During powerful rituals, their presence helps contain the raised energies, preventing dissipation and creating a potent, cohesive field.

Choosing a crystal is an intuitive process. Traditional correspondences can guide, but you trust your intuition and choose the stone that feels right for your current needs. You could hold different stones and notice which one resonates with you, creating a subtle pull or a sense of calm. Honour the crystals as the living, conscious partners they are. Trust your feelings: this is your intuition aligning with the crystal's energy to meet the particular needs of your ritual.
