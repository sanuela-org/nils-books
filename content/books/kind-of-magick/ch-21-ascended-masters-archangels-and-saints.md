# Ch 21 — Ascended Masters, Archangels and Saints

## What this chapter explores

For those who feel a resonance, ascended masters, archangels and saints can offer deep and compassionate support in the practice of [[magick|magick]]. These [[magickal-companions|magickal companions]] are invited into ritual as guides and helpers. Cherished companions from different traditions follow in three groups: ascended masters, archangels and saints.

## In essence

Having achieved enlightenment or Christ-consciousness, these beings serve humanity from a higher vibrational plane. They embody divine qualities such as healing, protection, wisdom, compassion and justice, often specialising in particular aspects. Working with them is not worship or begging: you align your intentions respectfully with their specific frequency of divine service.

Calling on one does not ask an external deity to grant a wish. You align your own energy with their perfected vibration, like tuning a musical instrument to a specific note. Their qualities then flow through you into the ritual, amplifying your focused intention.

The invitation happens at the gathering-companions step of the [[nine-step-manifesting-ritual|nine-step manifesting ritual]]: a heartfelt statement of the supportive presence you wish for. Their presence often manifests as a felt sense of peace, a sudden clarity, or a warm, expansive energy in the heart. Give as well as take; ask what they wish from you, and thank them afterwards.

These beings act according to divine will, always for the highest good of all. They mirror your soul's divine potential back to you and help you become a clear vessel through which harmonious change can occur.

Come with an open heart and pure intentions. The relationship is a personal communion, a union of consciousnesses dedicated to bringing more light, LOVE and healing into the world. Trust your intuition about which being you feel drawn to.
