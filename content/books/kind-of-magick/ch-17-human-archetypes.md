# Ch 17 — Human Archetypes

## What this chapter explores

Within each of us live [[archetypes|human archetypes]] — ancient blueprints of the soul: timeless roles, energies and qualities. We walk in their footsteps across many lifetimes. Some may feel like home to us; others may challenge us. Three for each of the four natural [[the-five-elements|elements]] form a strong and diverse foundation for [[magick|magick]]. A wider collection follows, then the King and the Queen.

## In essence

Each archetype is a flow of energy, a mirror rather than a confining mould. Gender here is symbolic: a man can embody the Mother's nurturing depth, a woman the Warrior's fierce clarity.

The archetype descriptions shows essence, symbols, shadow sides, traditional names and when to call on it. Together the profiles can balance active and receptive, light and shadow, internal and external, human and divine.

The Father, the Innocent, the Magician and more — each carry their own unique gifts. When one stirs something in you, that is often a sign of its value for your journey or cause. Life offers only what we are ready for. As we grow, each human archetype can reveal another layer of the deeper self. On the path towards liberation, they converge in the heart as integrated powers.

The King and the Queen are sovereign archetypes woven from threads of the others: a mature synthesis of all human archetypes. For many, they emerge in the final stages of transformation, just before full liberation, when the soul has remembered its centre and remains there. From that point, a human can rule over oneself, and that is the deepest magick of all.
