# Ch 6 — Practising Heart-Centred Magick

## What this chapter explores

[[magick|Heart-centred magick]] is practised consciously through a framework that is free in every sense of the word. It involves no rigid dogma or expensive tools, and can be adapted to your intuition and connection with the living world. The framework offers principles and practices to be experienced rather than a set of rules to be memorised.

## The perspective

The invitation is to reconnect with your innate power as a conscious co-creator and work in loving partnership with [[gaia|Gaia]], Mother Earth, to shape your [[personal-universe|personal universe]]. The roadmap first establishes the fundamentals: consciousness, energy and LOVE as the building blocks of reality, and aligning personal intention with the deeper divine flow. It then turns practical: preparing your energy and space, finding [[magickal-companions|magickal companions]], the [[nine-step-manifesting-ritual|Nine-Step Manifesting Ritual]] at the heart of the framework, and the [[healer-creator-technique|Healer & Creator Technique]] for deep inner transformation and energetic mastery.

The reference section ahead is intended to inspire and suggest possibilities rather than provide absolute definitions. The cultural and personal associations with these symbols are vast and varied. If you feel drawn to a symbol, element or guide that is not listed, or interpret a suggested archetype differently, honour that above all else. Your intuition is your trustworthy inner compass on this magickal journey.
