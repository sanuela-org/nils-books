# Ch 13 — Days of Full Indulgence

## What this chapter explores

The Soulful Sharing Friendship between Alistair and Fergus Brightstorm, Alchemist of the Mind from Altaris, turns a confession into a conversation about sobriety and sovereignty.

## In Essence

Fergus wants Alistair's transformation insights in a book on mindful eating and living. Over tea he asks, "I thought you had conquered all cravings?" Mostly, yes — but when cravings return temporarily, Alistair denies himself nothing and indulges fully "without the slightest regret". 

The cycle of indulgence and [[fasting-as-spiritual-practice|abstinence]] is a contrasting exercise — richness enjoyed, then simplicity and clarity with newfound gratitude.

Alistair describes how fasting clears his mind, removes the fog of overindulgence, makes him receptive to the moving energies of his body, the world and Goddess Sanuela — "like tuning a radio to a clearer frequency". It let him walk through the doors Calantha and Ayana opened, keeping his heart wide open for days.
