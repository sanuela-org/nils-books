# Ch 7 — A New Dawn in Altaris

## What this chapter explores

The terrain after liberation: what changes when cravings fall away and nourishment comes through energy rather than food. A forum stages the clash between traditions: cravings as thorns to be plucked, or cravings as energies to be transformed.

## The Message

With his cravings gone, Alistair feels as if he had tapped into a wellspring of astral energy nourishing him far more directly than any physical food. Sleep transforms: his body seems to revitalise itself with just four or five hours, often split into smaller naps followed by writing, sketching and conversation. His mind swirls with plans and the vision of his private home becoming a sanctuary, a safe space to explore the root causes of cravings. 

The term 'Thorns of Craving' becomes common locally and draws scholars and seekers across Sanuela; eastern visitors hold that cravings are energies to be transformed and channelled for growth rather than [[thorns-of-craving|thorns of craving]] to be plucked, and that avoiding them denies a part of oneself. Alistair answers through stories of transformation, and both sides leave with respect and a wider understanding.
