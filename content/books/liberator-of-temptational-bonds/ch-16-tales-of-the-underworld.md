# Ch 16 — Tales of the Underworld

## What this chapter explores

Back home, Alistair feels lifted by Fergus's words yet weighed down by tiredness, kin to the mythic heroes who descended into shadow worlds to face their inner demons. A poetic impulse seems to offer a mystical tale.

## In Essence

A song to himself plunges the seeker into the underworld as a search for higher frequencies hidden in the depths, until he finds his self-LOVE. He returns not as conqueror but as lover.

The Return has him facing lost souls and inner demons, resolving to build an Eden within his chest; he returns reborn, a hero to himself in his soul's grand tale. Writing lifts a weight: Alistair is both writer and hero of his own story, catching a glimmer of the self-LOVE and self-esteem that seemed distant.

Yet he must return to the underworld: fatigue, clouded mind, fading focus. Caffeine has its own addictive pull, and his promise to Fergus to stick exclusively to crystalglaze and a carbohydrate-rich diet anchors him. After the journey it will be easier to return to his [[fasting-as-spiritual-practice|fasting practice]].

He awaits the experiment's end, eager to guide others from the underworlds into the [[breatharian-initiation|astral-energetic heavens]]. The stars outside his window seemed to whisper: "You are on the right path, Alistair. Keep writing your story, for it is the ink and essence of your soul, and you have many chapters yet to fill."
