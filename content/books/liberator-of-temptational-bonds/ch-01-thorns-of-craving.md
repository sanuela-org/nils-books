# Ch 1 — Thorns of Craving

## What this chapter explores

The book opens with an excerpt from *Ayana, My Light and My Love*. Its subject is the [[thorns-of-craving|thorns of craving]]: entities that cling to souls with strong physical cravings and feed on the host. Ayana's hugs and heart-opening ceremonies of closeness can heal the wounds they leave.

## The Message

The thorns of craving are low vibrational astral entities, higher dimensional yet lower vibrational beings that feed themselves astral-energetically from a soul with strong physical cravings. They hook deeply into the host's astral body and bond to the ego, which becomes their astral energy generator and protector. Even a slight mention of dependency can make the tempted soul angry at those offering help.

Ayana's [[hugging-meditations|hugging meditations]] heal emotional and astral wounds, and the direct presence of her light is often enough to let unhealthy habits go. The thorns feel pain in her light and may revolt with anger or insults. Ayana reads these reactions as the thorns holding on and defending the host's ego, never the souls speaking from their hearts, and knows she is not the right one to help someone who is not ready. Such souls she sends to Alistair Rosevale, Liberator of Temptational Bonds from Altaris, who relieves toxic relationship bonds and physically induced cravings. The [[glossary#Great Change|Great Change]] banned the flavourful, physically addictive substances from local markets, and tempted souls greatly declined. Those who remain he helps by renewing the colonies of beneficial bacteria in their guts until cravings vanish, healing the emotional roots in parallel, until the entities depart and the soul is fueled with emotional freedom and self-LOVE.
