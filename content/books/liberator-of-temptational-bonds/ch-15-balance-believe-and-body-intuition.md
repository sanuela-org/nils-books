# Ch 15 — Balance, Believe, and Body Intuition

## What this chapter explores

After his deliberate return to crystalglaze, Alistair builds a body-guided balance plan around lemon water — and watches it collapse within days. A meeting with Fergus brings balance and a new understanding.

## The Message

After brief abstinence he feels more aware and creative — his balance improved — and returns to his usual [[glossary#Euphoric Crystalglaze|euphoric crystalglaze]] with a plan: lemon water at each blood-sugar spike. He knew the substances could not nourish, yet believed he could reduce the negative effects by trusting his body to tell him when he has had too much, when it is time to [[fasting-as-spiritual-practice|fast and cleanse]]. Confident he had a roadmap for recovery, he hoped for a healthier balance without rigid discipline.

Reality answers sooner: after a hefty lunch he is tired and drained; while Eirian visits her sister in the north, he feels depressed, bored and incredibly lonely. His mind promises one more candy or cake would bring the happiness he desired, but "the crystalglaze had other lessons to teach him."

Admitting to Fergus the plan had failed, Alistair relearns that mastering oneself is a lifelong task. True balance lies "not only in what I consume or avoid, but in my relationship with myself". He needs more patience, less harshness; if he feels unwell, so be it, it will dissolve again. Fergus thanks him for witnessing the struggles and triumphs and reveals the title of the new book.
