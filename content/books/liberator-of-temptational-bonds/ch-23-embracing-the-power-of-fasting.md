# Ch 23 — Embracing the Power of Fasting

## What this chapter explores

A [[guided-meditations-for-cravings|guided meditation]] preparing mind and soul for a [[fasting-as-spiritual-practice|regular fasting practice]] — settling worries about fasting before the first fast begins.

## In Essence

Grounding breaths let a radiant light descend through the centres of the body: spiritual wisdom at the crown, honest and loving communication at the throat, LOVE, compassion and courage at the heart centre, personal power and will at the solar plexus, grounding and safety at the root chakra.

A sacred space radiating LOVE, wisdom and divine energy opens, a beautiful place in nature if you like; at its centre stands an altar where worries and fears about fasting are placed one by one, acknowledged, and watched as they transform into golden light. Empowering images take their place — seeing yourself strong, capable, spiritually equipped — and a beam of light above the altar pours down the divine essence that supports future fasting journeys, easing all worries and cravings and increasing inner strength.

A gentle intention — physical detoxification, spiritual growth, increased energetic awareness, greater mental clarity, or all of these — is focused as a bright flame in the heart. Beside the altar, a pool of crystal clear water represents the purity and clarity that come from regular fasting: a hand dipped into it carries radiant white energy to the heart centre, where LOVE, wisdom and courage envelop the intentions. Two affirmations seal the preparation.
