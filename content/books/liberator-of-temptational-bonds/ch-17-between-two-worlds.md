# Ch 17 — Between Two Worlds

## What this chapter explores

The chapter carries the swing between two dietary worlds: a neighbour's ripe bananas pull Alistair toward balance, and the crystalglaze pulls him straight back again. 

## The Message

Cytherea Starlight brings Alistair bananas from her garden. Reading every event as a cosmic signpost, he takes the gift as a divine message from Goddess Sanuela — perhaps his venture into crystalglazed treats and carb-laden meals has reached its tipping point. 

The next morning a banana gives way to the crystalglaze again: remembering Fergus' assertion that willpower is a finite resource, he feels his daily quota spent — "possibly before he had even finished brushing his teeth".

The crystalglaze, he sensed, acts as a cap on his deeper feelings of LOVE, a sort of emotional comfort zone shielding him from confronting his deeper longings.

The experiment proves interesting, not without pleasures, but exhausting and draining; he wants a period of [[fasting-as-spiritual-practice|clean fasting]] to feel healthy, whole and awake again. The benefits arrive — alertness, creativity, intensified astral sensations, his heart space reconnected.

Another day with layered moonbread and extended dessert show he cannot stop eating without setting a clear limit. No substance could ever satisfy his true longings — what he seeks, like all humans, is LOVE, affection and understanding. He vows more fasting.
