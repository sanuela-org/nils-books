# Ch 6 — Being Guided to Let Go

## What this chapter explores

The turn from naming one's bondages to surrendering them: A spoken confession, an intuition he follows, and an enclave library. Its texts teach esoteric techniques for cutting the energetic cords tied to the thorns, often through mantras and sounds resonating at astral frequencies.

## In Essence

Alistair speaks his bondages aloud for the first time, and an intuition he chooses to trust pulls him north to Niramir. Calantha, a serene High Priestess connected to Goddess Sanuela, receives him with a long [[hugging-meditations|hugging meditation]]. His body first tenses, then vibrates and tingles; tears flow as if a dam had burst. Calantha confirms the astral entities clinging to him. She promises daily hugging ceremonies will keep him in loving divine superconsciousness, where no harmful astral beings can be around him. She advises gradually reducing food intake over the next three days, then [[fasting-as-spiritual-practice|fasting]] as a conscious affirmation of his spiritual essence.

During the fast, Alistair's cravings diminish, and a new nourishment arises: [[breatharian-initiation|strength drawn directly from the astral realms]]. He develops [[guided-meditations-for-cravings|guided meditations]] to help others understand the roots of cravings and prepare the mind and soul for fasting. He understands gut bacteria thriving on sugar are the physiological bullies shouting loudly. The roots of his dependencies are not just physiological but intimately intertwined with emotional voids and spiritual deficits.
