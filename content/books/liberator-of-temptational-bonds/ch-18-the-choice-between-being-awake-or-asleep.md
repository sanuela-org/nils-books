# Ch 18 — The Choice Between Being Awake or Asleep

## What this chapter explores

After Alistair's detours into indulgence, each craving opens a simple choice: give in and become inwardly dormant, or stay conscious and present in the heart. Where food cravings come from and what they comfort is explored through a conversation with Cerulean Starstrider.

## In Essence

When the neediness for food or substance arose, Alistair saw a simple choice: give in to indulgence and become inwardly dormant and less conscious, or stay highly energetic, highly conscious, fully present in his heart, honouring his connection with Mother Goddess Sanuela. Modest caffeine, dried and fermented green tea leaves combined with cacao, seemed to mimic [[fasting-as-spiritual-practice|fasting]]'s superconsciousness.

Over the next day-night cycle, his wakefulness shifted naturally higher, the whole world alive and conscious, everything connected and communicating with him. Yet the emotional pull toward food seemed unstoppable: no longer a physical addiction, he sensed, but the mental and emotional craving stayed.

Cerulean Starstrider, Guide to Enlightenment in Luminae, is asked why food still needs thought at all, and offers: "Could it be the fear of being fully awake? It seems to be the greatest fear a human being can experience, much more deeply rooted than the fear of leaving this body one day."

Asked what he is trying to comfort with food, Alistair answers: "It comes from my deep inner longing to be complete, divine Oneness." Cerulean urges him to use that longing. The journey follows the deepest longing for unity.
