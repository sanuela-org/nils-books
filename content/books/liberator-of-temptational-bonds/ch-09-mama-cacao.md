# Ch 9 — Mama Cacao

## What this chapter explores

One craving survives Alistair's freedom from all the rest: cacao, a natural plant medicine he relies greatly on to open his heart centre. He brews a healthy hot chocolate from oats and pure cacao powder, or simply eats 100% cacao chocolate, and recognises the cacao as only a substitute for his own lack of self-LOVE.

## In Essence

One craving remained after all the rest fell away. Alistair relied greatly on cacao as a natural plant medicine to open his heart centre further and further each day. His healthy hot chocolate starts with a tablespoon of oats boiled in water for a few minutes to achieve a rich, creamy texture. He strains the oat water and pours it over two tablespoons of pure cacao powder. No [[glossary#Euphoric Crystalglaze|euphoric crystalglaze]] or other artificial sweeteners are added, because the oat water already contains a hint of sweetness.

Cacao powder has been deprived of its healthy, rich and valuable cacao butter so that it lasts longer. He therefore adds either a spoonful of cacao butter or coconut oil. A little cinnamon and tiny, thin pieces of fresh ginger add flavour and additional health benefits.

At other times Alistair eats chocolate made from 100% cacao, including the precious cacao butter. This always opens his heart immediately and the mild caffeine content keeps him awake. The wakefulness serves him during the many hours of creative work on his projects. It also serves him while he guides his guests through their emotional processes related to cravings.

Alistair also recognised that cacao was only a substitute for his own lack of being one hundred percent in his deepest and most fulfilling self-LOVE.
