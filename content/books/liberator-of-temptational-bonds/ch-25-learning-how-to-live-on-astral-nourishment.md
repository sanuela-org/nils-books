# Ch 25 — Learning How to Live on Astral Nourishment

## What this chapter explores

The book's most radical invitation: learning to live on astral nourishment — a nine-day [[breatharian-initiation|Breatharian initiation experience]] in Tenerife toward living without food.

## In Essence

Living more and more on astral nourishment means drawing energy directly from the astral realms: you might eat once a day or once a week, then perhaps only for pleasure, energetic grounding and emotional balance — becoming more conscious, aware and awake. 

Those who follow the lifestyle often call themselves 'breatharians', but the term misleads — the breath is only part of the process. More important are the chakras, which connect the astral body to the physical; when the astral body goes food shopping in the astral realms, it gets everything it needs. For free.

The transition is a jump into cold water — learn to swim in the astral realms once. Some call it the Breatharian Initiation, in German *Lichtnahrungsprozess*, though it has nothing to do with sunlight, rather with higher frequencies. Safety is the condition: you need to feel physically and emotionally safe, so mind and brain can learn new pathways — "It will not happen if you believe you are starving."

The nine-day experience in Tenerife offers your own small apartment with bathroom and chill-out zone, walks in nature and energetic exercises for whatever emerges. There is no abrupt stop: food intake is gradually reduced over the first two days, a green smoothie is still there on the third morning. Then the six-day fast can begin. Less energy at first is common — usually a breakthrough follows, with much more energy than before. Full integration takes some months and may come in phases.
