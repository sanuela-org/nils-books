# Foreword

I know how you feel. I have been an addict for decades. Shortly after I was born, I began chasing food in general – and sugar, white carbs, and then coffee and alcohol. But addiction is not just about substances, it can be emotional and mental, and it can even be your favourite mobile game or someone you LOVE and want to 'own'. So many attachments in our lives!

Much of this book is about food, emotional cravings, dependencies and fasting. While writing and creating this book and a parallel non-fiction book of memes (*Balancing Emotional Needs: A New Relationship With Food*), I went through all the phases myself and fully embodied the experiences.

Coffee was the easiest addiction to get rid of. My reward was to be more in balance with my energetic rhythms. I could also feel the fine astral tingling inside me much better. Switching to green tea, then pu-erh tea, which is fermented green tea with less caffeine, was my way of gradually reducing the amounts without missing anything.

In a retreat with just myself and a dear friend as my host, I was intuitively guided to release any fears I had about letting go of the belief that my body needed any food at all. I was shown that this is actually the greatest addiction of all, acquired in our first moments of life and usually never questioned afterwards.

As I had never been able to fast for a single day before, this process was deeply cleansing, both physically and emotionally. My trusted friend helped me with [[hugging-meditations|hugging meditations]] and emotional support twice a day. Once again I experienced the power of these prolonged hugs with [[heart-centred-breathing|heart chakra breathing]]. Even the sometimes deeply felt detox pain in my bones and muscles disappeared.

I then went through long periods of integration, going through all sorts of further stages and processing. Techniques such as humming connect me to my astral body and transform limitless energies into my physical body when I need them. Many insects do the same.

Now I live in food freedom. Sometimes I eat once a day (usually mostly raw food), sometimes twice, sometimes not at all or only once a week – and I still maintain a high level of energy and with significantly higher frequencies because my body does not have to deal with the digestive process as much. Sometimes I eat a pizza with goat's cheese and feel absolutely fine, without the heaviness I used to feel. My body reacts differently to food now.

Repeatedly, I have many exciting experiences about my underlying craving 'for something' that is behind every desire for food or substances. I have found that in the end, we only want one thing: the most intimate and deepest union with ourselves. We want to find our deepest LOVE and our highest light. 💜

Much LOVE,
Nils
