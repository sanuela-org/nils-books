# Ch 11 — Emotional Neediness

## What this chapter explores

Alistair's view of emotional neediness as a barrier to growth — and Liliana, a guest nearly without hope of getting clean from euphoric [[glossary#Euphoric Crystalglaze|crystalglaze]].

## In Essence

Overly dependent souls hold themselves back from their own magical potential; each must find their own time to break free, relying on inner strength over constant validation. Some neediness is human and evolutionary, keeping us together — but when it becomes dependency or emotional bondage, it needs new ways to balance the underlying needs.

To awaken self-LOVE Alistair offers the meditative hugs Ayana has taught him. Guests improve step by step; neediness regularises and inner healing happens — when both breathe through their heart centres, magic and miracles often happen, their consciousness connected through the heart. He encourages self-LOVE, self-compassion and self-worth: taking really good care of oneself, expressing LOVE through [[hugging-meditations|hugging meditations]], keeping the heart centre vibrant and frequencies high.

He identifies underlying causes of cravings: past experiences, beliefs, behaviour patterns. Liliana struggled for years, traumatised as a child, still needy, seeking comfort in artificially sweetened food. She wanted loving comfort, peace and security; her mind had tied these wishes to being filled with certain foods. Her stepping stones were time, many long hugs, energetic transitions from Cerulean Starstrider, meetings with Ayana and Lana Lightweaver — step by step, she walked to inner freedom.
