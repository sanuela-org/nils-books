# Ch 20 — Healing Journey to the Roots of Cravings

## What this chapter explores

The first of the [[guided-meditations-for-cravings|guided meditations]]: a journey to the emotional roots of cravings for foods that may not be wholesome for the body, combining the wisdom of hypnotherapy with ancient spiritual practices.

## In Essence

The reader is guided into the body as the temple of the journey, breathing a light of trust and surrender through the third eye, then into the heart centre — "the core of your emotional and spiritual existence, a reservoir of infinite LOVE and infinite wisdom". From there, a path to the past opens, "paved with the cobblestones of memories, feelings and lessons".

Scenes may flood in or a single moment may crystallise; emotions — sadness, joy, regret, even indifference — are embraced as pieces of a puzzle, clues to the origins of cravings, dependencies and unfulfilled needs, with no need to judge or analyse. The Higher Self and the divine LOVE that connects us all hold the traveller; on the altar of the heart, the Inner Heart Light touches each remembered scene, and the thorns of craving and dependency begin to lose their grip.

The meditator comes back "not as you were when you began this meditation, but as someone closer to your true essence, radiant with newfound LOVE and understanding". 

An audio recording of the meditation accompanies the chapter.
