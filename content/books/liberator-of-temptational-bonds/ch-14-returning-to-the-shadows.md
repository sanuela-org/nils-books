# Ch 14 — Returning to the Shadows

## What this chapter explores

Walking through woods and meadows after the garden talk, Fergus proposes a book documenting several transformative journeys, beginning with Alistair's: the struggle, the discovery, the liberation, and the willing temporary return to crystalglaze dependency.

## In Essence

Tears well up as a deep astral tingle runs through Alistair, as if he has unlocked a forgotten prophecy. Like Ayana on her initiation, liberating himself anew creates a roadmap for others and possibly changes something energetically in the collective. Fergus's condition is strict: [[glossary#Euphoric Crystalglaze|euphoric crystalglaze]] only, nothing else that might interfere, a single focused experiment navigated with as much awareness as possible. 

On the second day, the numbing embrace wraps around him like a thick, suffocating blanket, and getting out of bed is a struggle. Squeezed lemon with water counteracts the haze; strong cacao and his special humming, making his pineal gland vibrate strongly, return a flicker of vitality. Doubt asks whether he was foolish to return to the trap. He reminds himself the descent is temporary, a necessary immersion for wisdom, and he is back among the sleepers he means to help.

After the first few days on crystalglazed sweets and one layered moonbread a day, the dullness and long-unfelt anxiety make a [[fasting-as-spiritual-practice|fasting]] day feel like real relief. The evening ends in the slippery slope he'd feared: first just fruit, then biscuits. The substance boils in his veins; he feels a conflicting mixture of feeling incredibly alive and uneasy.
