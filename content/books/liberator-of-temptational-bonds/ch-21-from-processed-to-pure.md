# Ch 21 — From Processed to Pure

## What this chapter explores

A [[guided-meditations-for-cravings|guided meditation that shifts cravings]] from processed foods to whole fruits and natural foods — reframing what the body asks for through imagery and affirmation.

## In Essence

The meditative journey turns to the foods you gravitate towards when cravings strike — convenient and instantly gratifying, perhaps, but lacking nutritional depth.

The reframing is a sensory experience, and the release deepens the commitment to honouring the body. Three affirmations anchor the shift. A final visualisation walks the reader past old craved foods to fresh fruit beside them, and the meditation closes into "a new chapter in your life, one of mindful, joyful eating, where every choice you make is an act of LOVE for your body and soul."
