# Ch 5 — Discovery of the Thorns

## What this chapter explores

Sleepless nights over his family's discord drive Alistair into the library of Altaris, where whispers of a hidden chamber — myth to some, sealed away for a reason to others — draw him beyond refuge. The discovery is made with a soft click and the swinging of a bookshelf to reveal a dust-covered scripture about the *Thorns of Craving*.

## In Essence

The chaotic atmosphere at home broke Alistair's wall of denial, leaving him open to unconventional ways of understanding. The library's enchantments admitted only the truly dedicated or desperate; he was both. He absorbed the story of an ancient scholar, a researcher of esoteric knowledge, who shielded his writings from sceptics seeking to destroy them. Alistair felt a strange resonance with the scholar, as if their souls were bound by the same thirst for understanding.

*Thorns of Craving* describes that astral beings known as [[thorns-of-craving|thorns]] cling to humans and influence their weaknesses and cravings. The scripture mirrored his own life so closely that he felt a strong astral tingle run up his spine. For the first time, Alistair considered that his struggle might involve forces beyond family dysfunction and societal pressures.

Scholars and spiritual healers of Altaris met the passages with a mixture of scepticism and curiosity. The healers recalled mentions of astral entities in their own esoteric studies, though none could confirm the thorns exist. All advised caution about the spiritual and emotional turmoil such an investigation might cause. The mixed reception kindled Alistair's fire instead of damping it; the veil was lifted, and he knew he couldn't turn back.
