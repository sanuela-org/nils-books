# Ch 8 — When the Thorns Kept a Soul Alive

## What this chapter explores

Alistair's work with guests whose cravings are entangled with their will to live, and the patience his work demands. Readiness cannot be forced; higher-frequency substitutes and [[heart-centred-breathing|heart-centred breathing]] are the first footholds a guest can reach.

## The Message

Through guests who are depressed, overworked, traumatised or otherwise troubled, Alistair learns that many need their cravings and thorns to go on living. If you took the substances away from some of them, they would have little reason to live. So he waits until a guest is really ready. For some this comes after days of inner crisis or a low frequency hole; for others, when they are fully saturated with their preferred substance. Several attempts are normal; guests bounce between balance, freedom and LOVE and times of falling back.

Most remain addicted to some food or substance but switch to higher-frequency ones: pure cacao and organic foods and herbs. When a craving comes up, a hand on the heart centre with focused [[heart-centred-breathing|heart centre breathing]] raises the feeling of self-LOVE. Alistair suggests a green smoothie in the morning and lemon or vinegar water, keeping the body alkaline for a higher astral frequency and healing inflammation.

These are the first beginnings of reaching a soul not yet aware how much the thorns control their ego-mind and life. Only after finding freedom from physical addictions can that power be understood.
