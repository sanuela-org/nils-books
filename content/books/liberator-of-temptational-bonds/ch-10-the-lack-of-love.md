# Ch 10 — The Lack of Love

## What this chapter explores

Through his personal introspection, observation and life experience, Alistair had found various motives for people to turn to food. Many were plagued by the thorns, their choices dominated by astral beings and gut bacteria that thrive on [[glossary#Euphoric Crystalglaze|euphoric crystalglaze]] and other unhealthy and addictive substances. 

## In Essence

Behind the bite of something sweet there is often "an unspoken cry for something more: connection, acceptance and LOVE" — a disconnection from self-LOVE. The hunger for LOVE seeks solace in edible comfort, but food can never truly fill that emotional void: it serves as an emotional crutch.

The lack extends beyond the plate: neglected relationships, unfulfilled potential, settling for mediocrity. Every crystalglazed snack chosen over one's true feelings adds an unaddressed layer.

Food's seduction is fleeting: it occupies the body while an eternal hunger rooted in the soul stays.

Food can only temporarily satisfy the emotional body; LOVE nourishes the soul. The cycle of longing and eating deepens disconnection from the innermost heart centre. Like covering a leaking roof with a blanket: the problem returns.

Alistair broke free on the journey north, in the enclave near Niramir with Calantha's help: he had to LOVE himself far more deeply than he ever thought possible. The key was neither a magic pill nor a revolutionary diet, but the courageous act of turning inwards.

He advocates mindful eating — savouring every bite, understanding the source and purpose of your food — and soul-enriching activities in food's place. When no one else says it, he repeats to himself: "Alistair, I LOVE you!". Addressing root causes and pursuing soul-satisfying alternatives, he believed, could free anyone from the imprisoning cycle.
