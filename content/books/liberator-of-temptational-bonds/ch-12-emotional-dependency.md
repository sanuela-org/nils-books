# Ch 12 — Emotional Dependency

## What this chapter explores

Nyvii, a friend of many years, comes as a Soulful Sharing Friend — a bond between equals, built on giving, receiving and learning — asking help with emotional dependencies and cravings.

## In Essence

Alistair knew her fight with self-LOVE and self-worth: validation, happiness and fulfilment must be cultivated within, not sought from others. Her cravings were heightened; unsure she wanted to live without the substances that made life acceptable.

He gave her LOVE and [[hugging-meditations|hugging meditations]] with heart-centred breathing, as Ayana had shown him — a soul long without closeness carries a hole to fill. His closeness left her wanting more, so they talked: she felt connected and complete beside him, yet not ready for a relationship.

They held hands as they talked, a mini hug. Alistair proposed a sphere of light of almost unconditional LOVE, with one condition: reduced intimacy. They soon began a committed, loving freedom with an extended [[moon-love-relationship|Moon Love relationship]], celebrating a sacred, intimate LOVE ceremony each lunar cycle, having personal allowances and boundaries between those times.
