# Ch 22 — Improving Your Relationship With Food

## What this chapter explores

A [[guided-meditations-for-cravings|guided meditation for improving the relationship with food]] by giving your inner parts a voice — a roundtable dialogue that turns cravings into information about needs.

## In Essence

The heart becomes a serene room with a welcoming round table covered with the foods of daily life: those that respect body and soul, and the emotional crutches that offer some satisfaction, but stay hollow and unbalanced.

The inner parts are invited to sit at the table. The part most vocal about unhealthy food is asked, "Why do you crave what you crave?" We listen without judgement for the purpose behind it: protect, comfort, or avoid? The part seeking nourishment and health is asked, "What do you need to feel truly fulfilled?" The dialogue continues; perhaps the craving part finds wisdom in the nourishing part, a meeting in the middle. Each part is thanked: all have a role in making you who you are.

The closing affirmation — "I am the loving guardian and caretaker of my own nourishment" — sends the traveller back through a counted awakening.
