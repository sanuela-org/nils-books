# Ch 26 — Creative Shamanic Ritual for Cleansing

## What this chapter explores

Intuitive, creative shamanic rituals for couples to cleanse the aura of both participants from bad spirits and any cravings, the book answers with eight small ritual scripts.

## In Essence

The rituals use crystals, stones, natural objects and ordinary household items to symbolically and energetically cleanse the aura of both participants from negative energies and cravings. Setting the sacred space clears a quiet room where the couple sits facing each other, a small altar or cloth at the centre. Cleansing with smoke waves smoldering dried herbs — sage, cedar or palo santo — around bodies and space from the feet upward, with the intention to release negative energies or attachments. A crystal grid arranges several resonant crystals in a circle around the altar, a larger one at the centre as focal point.

Personal tokens — found objects from nature holding symbolic meaning, representing intentions, desires or aspects to release or transform — are gathered by each participant. In the symbolic offering, a dish of salt and water symbolises purification and cleansing; the tokens are placed into it as a gesture of release. Chanting and mantras: holding hands, the couple chants a sacred word, an intuitive humming, or mantra that resonates — the vibrations and intentions behind the sound cleanse and purify the energy fields. Energy balancing massages each other's hands, feet or backs while visualising the energy flow, intuition focusing on blocked or imbalanced areas to release stagnant energy or cravings. Gratitude and closing thanks the experience, each other and the elements, extinguishes the herbs and releases any residual energy.

The book's caveat: these rituals are meant to be personalised and adapted — trust your intuition and feel free to modify or add elements that resonate.
