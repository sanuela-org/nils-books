# Ch 24 — Just Imagine ...

## What this chapter explores

A vision essay about an imagined world where most of what graces our tables truly deserves the label "food". Such nourishment ripples outward, from children's emotional lives to society's health, productivity and even collective consciousness.

## The Message

The outdated principle of separating chemical ingredients from their natural surroundings (for example, extracting sugar from the whole fruit) seems to lead only to disbalance, illness and addiction. The new world values the integrity and harmony of all its parts, their fibres and nutrients still fully intact and interconnected. 

Food labels would read differently: no unpronounceable chemicals, no degree in biochemistry needed, just a list of whole, recognisable foods. 

Effects reach beyond physical health. Children growing up without the constant highs and lows of sugar-laden foods could develop a more balanced emotional life, potentially lowering the incidence of mental health issues like anxiety and depression.

A society free from food-based addictions would likely see decreased healthcare costs, increased productivity and perhaps even more harmonious relationships at home and in the workplace. Imagine a collective rise in spiritual and emotional intelligence, born of this nourishment and wholeness; the positive impact this could have on societal issues is "beyond measure."
