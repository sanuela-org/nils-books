# Foreword

## What this chapter explores

After the traditional model fell apart, the author searched for a new way to live relationships. The foreword explains why he wrote this book.

## Key Insight

After his last traditional partnership fell apart, an inner voice told him that was the last of its kind. He must find the new way. He first rebelled, longing for a partner yet fearing the system, anxiety and high sensitivity making it harder. Yet not a single date came of it.

The traditional model was enforced by warlords and popes and anchored over centuries into the collective consciousness. Media and surroundings constantly reprogram it; any exit seems nearly impossible.

He rejected the open relationship label and the view that jealousy is each person's responsibility. The urge to see one single soul as a perfect counterpart inevitably fails. Expectation becomes too great. Sensual connection grows dull. Deep honesty erodes — even with ourselves — as fear of losing a partner shapes who we are.

People adapt without noticing, only rediscovering themselves after separation. His real need as a mammal is stability not questioned every single day — trust, honesty, cohesion, and the shared carrying of challenges.

The book offers stable relationship networks and the skills to become fully human. In the coming age of robotics, the time for this has never been more urgent. All are warmly welcome at Finca Sanuela.
