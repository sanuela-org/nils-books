# Ch 9 — Surrender and Honesty

## What this chapter explores

Mark arrives at Chloe's carrying regret after a fight with Maia. Honesty, surrender and confession surface old wounds. Core Practice 2 — the Planning Pause — emerges, and a tentative step toward a shared network.

## In Essence

Mark replays the fight on the drive over. He announced plans, did not ask, took Maia for granted. At Chloe's door he hides nothing and explains the scheduling failure and unspoken expectations that bred resentment, without defending himself. She suggests sitting together for tea — not separate pairs, but a web of honest connections.

The evening deepens into quiet intimacy. Chloe surrenders into his presence. He carries contentment alongside a question: how would Maia feel?

He tells her everything. Her face stays calm, but the flicker catches even her by surprise. Wound: it is not about Chloe. If someone else makes him happy, perhaps she is afraid she is not special anymore.

Mark's instinct is to defend, explain, solve. He stops. He breathes. He listens. He apologises for what happened and for any pain his excitement caused. Then he asks: is there anything you need from me?

Maia names what must change — stop making assumptions. Mark writes down Core Practice 2: the Planning Pause. The jealousy doesn't vanish; it becomes something they can work through together. Chloe's group-tea idea is accepted — you cannot build a Network of LOVE by avoiding things. The notebook opens to a fresh page.

## Practice

Core Practice 2: The Planning Pause. Before committing to a plan involving someone else, pause and check in with your partner — not for permission, but for transparency. A ten-second message prevents scheduling surprises. For significant firsts, allow a twenty-four-hour waiting period so emotions settle. When an invitation arises, practise the shared glance: look at your partner to silently check in before responding.
