# Ch 8 — Plans and Expectations

## What this chapter explores

A daily heart-share steadies Maia and Mark as their relationship opens. A new connection with Chloe brings joy. Then a scheduling crash reveals the real scarcity: time and energy, not jealousy, are a network's most finite resource.

## The Message

Their heart-share practice works for a week. A crash follows: Mark accepts a Thursday with Chloe without asking Maia. She swallows her disappointment — "It's fine" — assuming her time was the flexible variable. Both use blame and defence instead of the skills they already know. The crash came from a failure to manage their shared time.

Time and energy management is the real work. Relationship bandwidth is finite and requires conscious, transparent communication — not unlimited expansion. Co-created schedules and shared calendars reduce anxiety. Quality time means full presence; self-care and solo time are non-negotiable. Scheduling conflicts are navigated as inevitable events, not personal rejections. Conflict repair means de-escalating with honest, loving communication and apologising for consequences rather than just explaining intentions. Conflicts send ripples through the wider network. Healthy mediation holds space; triangulation pulls a third party to take sides or avoid direct communication. The distinction matters.

## Practice

A short daily heart-share, five to ten minutes, builds trust and emotional transparency. Sit or exchange voice messages. Breathe through your heart centres. Share openly and informally: gratitude, a worry, or background imbalance. Speak from the heart with "I feel" statements. Listen to understand, not to respond. No need to solve — acknowledging is enough. End with a quiet moment or a hug. Honesty and consistency matter more than perfect days.
