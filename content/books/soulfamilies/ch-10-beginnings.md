# Ch 10 — Beginnings

## What this chapter explores

The search begins with a shared walk-through of an old farmhouse on a hill. Someone new is joining the group, and together, they check on [[clarifying-space-needs|what a shared space must support]].

## The message

The farmhouse looks simple and holds charm — stone walls, red tile roof, chickens on the porch. Kai brings Elina, a friend who blogs about new ways of living and has a young son. Walking through the house together helps them figure out what they want: away from separate rooms with single beds, toward multipurpose spaces that serve and stay private when needed — though most rooms here are too small for it. Over tea at Ari's, Elina brings a warm, grounded presence, and the open questions turn serious: renting together, space and responsibilities, privacy, conflict. They decide to spend more time together first, slowly building a foundation of trust and togetherness.

A space must carry warmth and possibility, and fit the rhythm of shared living — the most beautiful place can bring daily frustration otherwise. What will the space be used for? Which features are must-haves, which nice-to-have? Who needs more solitude, who more shared space? Which rooms become central — kitchen, living room, music corner, quiet retreats? Private rooms or flexible multipurpose spaces? How do layout and movement flow? And can the place grow with a yurt, a studio, or a guest hut as the group changes?
