# Ch 3 — Connection

## What this chapter explores

Closeness with a safe structure: pairs breathe hand in hand while a third holds space. The supporter experiences compersion (the joy of seeing others connect).

## The message

In groups of three, two sit face to face, knees close, eyes closed, [[inner-sanctuary#Practice: Heart-centred breathing|breathing through the heart centre]]; left palm up to receive, right palm down to give. The [[touch-as-connection#Touch in a trio|third becomes the spaceholder]]: a hand on each, not sending anything, just breathing LOVE and being present. In that seat you might feel [[non-attachment-and-compersion|compersion]] — a deep joy as you support others. For Ari and Jaro, the exercise becomes the first real connection: breathing, presence, and the feeling of not being alone, of not having to hold anything back.

Compersion is often described as the opposite of jealousy. Instead of focusing on insecurity or fear of loss, we feel joy when others experience connection, happiness and fulfilment. It is about [[abundance-and-scarcity-in-love|expanding our capacity to LOVE]]: others stop being rivals and become part of a shared field of connection. The supporting role itself becomes deeply fulfilling — and with touch as its language, a held hand can open the same door as an embrace.
