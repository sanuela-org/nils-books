# Ch 1 — Arrival

## What this chapter explores

The threshold: a guarded woman steps into an unfamiliar retreat. One shared breath in a circle of strangers is enough to open her heart.

## In essence

Ari nearly turns back. The retreat email sat in her inbox for weeks — too big, too open, too unknown — until a quiet yes rises from her heart. In a garden of wooden houses, phones go into a basket and strangers sit together in a yurt. Jaro, Kai and Nila arrive one by one. Siona opens the circle with the [[inner-sanctuary#Practice: Heart-centred breathing|simplest breathing technique]] she knows.

You breathe naturally and imagine the air flowing through your heart centre instead of your nose — LOVE in, LOVE out. A hand on the centre of the chest deepens the feeling if you want it. Nothing needs changing; the breath simply flows. The practice calms the nervous system, gathers attention into the body, opens the heart, supports honest and caring communication, and keeps you [[emotional-self-regulation|emotionally grounded]]. For Ari, something begins to shift: a door inside her opens.

## Practice: Heart-centred breathing

The retreat's first teaching, offered before anything else: breathe naturally and imagine the air flowing through your heart centre — LOVE in, LOVE out. See [[inner-sanctuary|inner sanctuary]] for the complete practice.
