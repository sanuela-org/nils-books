# Ch 15 — A Few Days That Change Everything

## What this chapter explores

From reading to living: the invitation to experience what a soulfamily could feel like, at Finca Sanuela in Tenerife.

## In essence

Reading about building a [[soulfamily-community|soulfamily community]] is one thing. At [[sanuela-retreats|Finca Sanuela]], a few days or weeks are shaped entirely around where you currently are — alone, with a partner, or as a small group already exploring the idea together. There is no fixed programme and no pressure to be anywhere other than exactly where you are.

What you can practise and explore there: [[inner-sanctuary#Practice: Heart-centred breathing|heart-centred breathing]] and sharing circles, your own vision for community living, somatic practices like eye gazing, hand-holding and [[hugging-meditations|hugging meditations]], compersion and holding space, [[shadow-work|shadow work]], practical community skills from shared decisions to a living [[soulfamily-compass|soulfamily compass]] — and one-to-one time with Nils. What makes this different from a workshop: it is immersive, somatic, personalised, and Finca Sanuela itself lives the open-hearted togetherness the book describes. Retreat lengths run from a first taste of a few days to deeper immersion and stays beyond two weeks. If it resonates, the next step is simple: come and experience it for yourself.
