# Ch 6 — Networks

## What this chapter explores

The word arrives: relationship networks. Siona describes how they form and hold, and the forest's underground mycelium shows the same pattern in nature.

## The message

When we stop limiting ourselves to being single or a couple, we can build connections with care — personal [[allowances-and-boundaries|allowances and clear boundaries]], set with the open knowing and acceptance of everyone's closer partners. A [[networks-of-love|Network of LOVE]] then forms naturally, through trust, presence and shared experience. Stability and depth count more than the excitement of new beginnings: when challenges arise, [[welcoming-a-third-soul|another trusted soul]] steps in, and what already exists is nurtured with patience and LOVE.

The mycelium gives the model its biology. Trees and plants are connected underground, sharing nutrients and distress signals, making the whole forest stronger by freely nourishing. Relationships can grow the same way: staying connected without constant presence, supporting with presence alone, sharing care and resources freely, sensing who needs help, protecting the vulnerable, building trust where no part dominates, growing slowly and organically, healing through being lovingly seen and supported. No small boxes, no ownership — openness with care, honesty and trust.
