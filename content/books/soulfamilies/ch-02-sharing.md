# Ch 2 — Sharing

## What this chapter explores

The sharing circle as the retreat's grounding base: honest speaking from the heart centre, full-presence listening, and the experiences that led the retreat members to question the traditional couple dynamic they grew up with.

## The message

Siona sets the rules: speak while breathing through your [[heart-centred-communication|heart centre]], listen with full presence — no advice, no fixing, no judging. Honest sharing emerges: a marriage in which one partner felt unseen; a young man who wondered if there was something wrong with him; and a revolving door of relationships that always ended in the same way. Ari describes her experience: she followed all the familiar rules, but something always felt off — as if she were playing a part instead of living her life as herself.

The [[monogamy|traditional family model]] gets weighed directly. A couple, one household, sometimes children: it brings structure, acceptance, stability and legal security. It also assumes one human can meet nearly all emotional and physical needs, breeds emotional dependency and fear of loss, isolates couples from wider support, and can create resentment or loneliness even inside the partnership. When the circle ends holding hands, a gentle trust has formed, like a first layer of soil in which something new could grow.
