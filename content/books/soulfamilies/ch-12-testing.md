# Ch 12 — Testing

## What this chapter explores

A trial month under one roof: shared sleeping, named roles, first ruptures and repairs — and the [[shared-decision-making|decision-making models]] that keep a small group flowing.

## The message

Elina's cosy flat hosts the experiment. Each evening the living room becomes a mattress-and-duvet landscape — a [[clarifying-space-needs|shared nest]], phones put away. The book footnotes a blog post on shared sleeping here: *Why We Think Sleeping in Isolated Rooms Is Normal* (sanuela.org/sharing-sleeping-place).

Day six brings tensions: Kai's worry about Nila and Jaro, wrapped as concern for the group; Ari's planning against Elina's drift; a kind remark on an old parenting wound. In the evening circle, Ari names her tiredness — "I want to be part of the whole, not the one holding it together" — and Jaro names [[group-dynamics-in-a-relationship-network|unconscious roles]]: "We can choose again every day." Check-ins follow every other day.

One member decides after gathering ideas; inviting someone new needs everyone. Consensus values full alignment but slows things down; advice-based brings clarity — an initiator listens, then chooses; fluid leadership follows whoever feels clear and needs high awareness. Light sociocracy decides by consent, not consensus: not "Do you agree?" but "Is this proposal good enough for now? Is it safe enough to try? Or do you feel strong resistance against it?" The method matters less than the intention: say things clearly, listen also to tone and body, speak up early.
