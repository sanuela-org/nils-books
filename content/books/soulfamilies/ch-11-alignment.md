# Ch 11 — Alignment

## What this chapter explores

The inner workings of the group involve people sorting through longing, fear and honesty on their own. The non-fictional part provides shared ground through common values and a process for making agreements.

## The message

Jaro is drawn to both Ari and Nila, and feels a greater sense of caring and LOVE for them. Ari's flat becomes a visioning workshop; what she longed years to say out loud clicks into place. Kai is proud of how he has learned to handle his emotions. Jealousy still surfaces occasionally, but he is no longer afraid of it. He realised that LOVE doesn't diminish when shared — it grows when held with care. Nila's dreams turn vivid; Elina sits between the man she has been seeing, the honest spark with Kai, and her deeper longing: shared meals, holding hands, long hugs, her son growing up in a small caring group.

Honesty gets turned into structure. Agreements are not strict rules but a [[soulfamily-compass|living compass]] that grows and adapts. The values strong groups share: transparency, mutual care, support in LOVE without possession, emotional responsibility, freedom, respect for space, commitment to growth, shared intentions, celebrating differences, staying with the process. The compass process shapes them together: reflect alone, listen in small circles, come together with the full group, and make a mindmap for the wall — values in the centre, examples around them, updated as needed. Let it live and breathe with you.
