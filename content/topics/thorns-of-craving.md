# Thorns of Craving

## Definition

The thorns of craving are low vibrational astral entities that feed themselves astral-energetically from a soul with strong physical cravings. They hook deeply into the host's astral body and bond to the ego, which becomes their astral energy generator and protector. The book [[liberator-of-temptational-bonds|Liberator of Temptational Bonds]] describes them as the source of addiction.

## How the thorns hold on

The thorns hook in where cravings are strong, and even a slight mention of dependency makes the tempted soul angry at those offering help — the hook and the placed sensitivities at work. Within the sphere of a strong light of LOVE they feel pain and revolt with anger without reason, peculiar interpretations and insults, to prove their way of life is the only way to remain in control. None of this is the soul speaking from the heart. It is the thorns holding on and using as well as defending the host's ego, afraid of losing their astral food provider.

## What feeds them

Strong physical cravings are their nourishment, fed astral-energetically from the host. From the body side, [[glossary#Euphoric Crystalglaze|euphoric crystalglaze]] (what we call sugar in our world) and other physically addictive substances feed a very demanding kind of gut bacteria that thrive on these substances. 

Behind every craving stands a lack of self-LOVE: what the snack promises — comfort, peace, security, the feeling of being loved — is sought outside, where it can only be cultivated within. Self-LOVE fills the spaces the cravings try to fill, and a heart centre kept vibrant leaves the thorns nothing to feed on.

## What helps

Mindful eating, savouring every bite and understanding the source and purpose of your food can make them lose their grip. [[fasting-as-spiritual-practice|Fasting]] raises your frequencies and starves the thorns of craving. Liberation removes what feeds them. 

Renewed gut bacteria through fermented and wholesome foods can take the body side away; healed emotional wounds and a heart centre kept vibrant through [[heart-centred-breathing|heart-centred breathing]] and [[hugging-meditations|hugging meditations]] help on the astral side. 

When self-LOVE awakens, they have nothing left to hold on to. The thorns then often leave in search of other souls at lower frequencies.

The [[guided-meditations-for-cravings|guided meditations for cravings]] walk you down to the roots directly.
