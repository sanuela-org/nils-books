# Glossary

The words of these books carry precise meanings. This glossary gathers them across all books, each with a short description in the author's own understanding — the place to check what a term means wherever you meet it.

## Geborgenheit

The German word for a deep sense of comfort, belonging, safety and emotional warmth — being perfectly held and utterly safe. It does not come from locked doors or high walls, but from a soothing internal radiance. The full practice lives on the [[inner-sanctuary|Inner Sanctuary]] page.

## Heart-centred breathing

The master practice at the centre of this work: breathing LOVE in and out through the centre of your chest, as though your heart itself were breathing. It instantly pulls your awareness out of the mind and into the calm, intuitive space of the heart. You can do it anywhere, in any moment. Nearly every other practice builds on it, from emotional self-regulation and communication to deep surrender. The full practice lives on the [[inner-sanctuary|Inner Sanctuary]] page.

## Hugging meditation

A sustained, consensual embrace held with full presence — the strongest form of human medicine we can share. Steps and variations live on the [[hugging-meditations|Hugging Meditations]] page.

## Inner Patriarch

The voice in the head that perpetuates the imbalance of a controlling world, regardless of gender: the strict inner supervisor that manages feelings, prioritises efficiency and achievement over wellbeing, and treats the heart's openness as a threat. It mistakes a fortress for a home. Part of [[masculine-and-feminine-balance|masculine and feminine balance]].

## Inner protector

The mind's innate survival mechanism, often called the ego: it guards a set of core fears and builds walls to keep pain out. Its work deserves gratitude even while its strategies cost us. The full picture lives on the [[inner-protector|Inner Protector]] page.

## Luma

Luma is the inner presence of light and LOVE. It begins as a subtle, heart-centred glow, stirring when trust opens the way and truth is felt in the heart, allowing divine LOVE to flow. As soft as candlelight and as steady as the sun, Luma speaks in whispers, but always tells the truth. When welcomed, it grows into a radiant warmth that fills the whole body, like light pouring through every cell.

## Moon Love relationship

A bond between two close friends, based on deep trust and care, that comes together in a more sensual way only once per lunar cycle — for example by cuddling naked or exploring each other's bodies sensually. Named in [[soulfamilies|Soulfamilies]] among the new possibilities beyond the couple model.

## Network of LOVE

A stable web of several caring relationships that share the emotional load a single partner would otherwise carry alone, held together by honesty, consent and trust. The model and its skills live on the [[networks-of-love|Networks of LOVE]] page.

## Reparenting

[[meeting-fear|Meeting your fears]] and vulnerability the way a good parent meets a child: with acknowledgement, steadiness and care. Walking through fear this way teaches the nervous system that vulnerability is not an overwhelming threat, and that the heart can guide more confidently than the ego's alarms.

## Soulfamily community

A small circle of trusted souls as a chosen family who shares daily life, care and growth. It grows like the mycelial network that feeds a forest rather than like the closed couple box, and its agreements take shape as a [[soulfamily-compass|living compass]]. Developed in [[soulfamilies|Soulfamilies]]; the full page lives at [[soulfamily-community|Soulfamily Community]].

## Spaceholder

The third soul in a trio exercise who supports a connected pair: a hand on each of the two, not sending anything, just breathing LOVE and being present. It is the seat where [[non-attachment-and-compersion|compersion]] can be felt. Introduced in [[soulfamilies|Soulfamilies]]; the role in full lives on [[welcoming-a-third-soul|Welcoming a Third Soul]].

## Yab yum

A shared meditation position: one partner sits in the other's lap and wraps their legs around them, eyes closed, breathing through the heart centre — no goal, just being together, present and open. Described in [[soulfamilies|Soulfamilies]] within the hugging meditation; the practice lives on the [[hugging-meditations|Hugging Meditations]] page.

## Yang Dictatorship

The term was used in [[confident-heart|The Confident Heart]] to describe millennia of imbalanced masculine principles, where separation, control and domination were prioritised over connection. Ending it begins within, by rebalancing masculine and feminine energies. Part of [[masculine-and-feminine-balance|masculine and feminine balance]].
