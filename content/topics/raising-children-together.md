# Raising Children Together

## Definition

Raising children together in a [[soulfamily-community|soulfamily community]] means children grow up held by a circle of trusted adults rather than only by their parents. In a caring network, there are more people to LOVE them and more hands for daily life. It offers a way of life in which children can breathe freely and grow into trust and joy.

## The circle of trusted adults

Raising children together in a small circle of trusted souls is not inventing anything new. For most of human history, children grew up held by a group of caring adults, not just by two overburdened parents behind closed doors. In a soulfamily community, where people share their daily lives, children are cared for collectively. Each adult brings something different, and together they form a web of safety, inspiration and LOVE. It is another type of loving network that does not involve ownership.

## Benefits for parents and the soulfamily

The book [[soulfamilies|Soulfamilies]] describes it like this:

- The load of parenting is shared, parents can take time off to rest, recover or work, knowing their child is loved and supported.
- Soulfamily members without children can still play a meaningful role in a child's life.
- Everyone learns from the joys and challenges of being around children.
- Emotional balance is easier to maintain when we are not alone in difficult moments.
- Children bring fresh energy, playfulness, and learning to the whole community.
- Stronger bonds form between all adults as they care for and protect something together.

## Benefits for the children

- Children feel seen and loved by more than just one or two souls.
- They naturally learn to trust and connect with different kinds of adults.
- More emotional and practical needs are met.
- They grow up with a sense of belonging and togetherness.
- They witness and participate in honest, authentic adult relationships around them.
- They are less likely to feel isolated or burdened by their parents' struggles.
- They carry a deep memory within them: *LOVE is shared, support is natural, and I am never really alone*.
