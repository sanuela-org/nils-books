# Guided Meditations for Cravings

## Definition

A set of guided meditations for releasing the emotional roots of food cravings. Each journey opens the heart centre, lets old feelings and memories surface to be met with LOVE, and acts as an energetic medicine for the soul — moving you towards a purer diet, a more mindful relationship with food, and a life less ruled by cravings.

## Practices

[Listen to the audio recordings of these guided meditations here.](https://www.sanuela.org/thankyou/#Guided_Meditations_for_Food_Cravings)

- **Healing Journey to the Roots of Cravings**: An introductory journey into the heart centre and down the "path to your past, paved with the cobblestones of memories, feelings and lessons" — emotions embraced as puzzle pieces, the Inner Heart Light touching each scene until the [[thorns-of-craving|thorns]] lose their grip. 
- **From Processed to Pure**: craved foods shrunk in the mind's eye to grey, tasteless images; fruits materialised large and vibrant; three affirmations; leading towards mindful, joyful eating, where every choice you make is an act of LOVE for your body and soul.
- **Improving Your Relationship With Food**: the heart as a room with a round table where the various inner parts are asked "Why do you crave what you crave?" and "What do you need to feel truly fulfilled?"; imagined mindful eating; two sealing affirmations. 
- **Embracing the Power of Fasting**: a light descending through the centres of the body, worries about fasting transformed on an altar, a pool of crystal clear water, and the affirmation "I am ready to embrace the purifying power of fasting."
