# Transforming the Longing

## Definition

Longing is the soul's guidance system. The ache for something more points at qualities you are ready to embody yourself. Transforming the longing means turning the search outward (for the perfect partner who could finally complete you) into a practice of becoming what you seek.

## Longing as guidance

What you long for in others maps to capacities alive in you, waiting to be lived. The intense pull you feel towards a potential partner rarely concerns them alone. It reflects back disowned qualities and forgotten parts of your soul, asking to be integrated.

You are drawn to these qualities not because you lack them, but because you possess them within you. Your soul uses the attraction as a trigger to awaken what is already there. A true partner is a loving witness and source of encouragement, not a substitute for your wholeness.

The turning point is embracing the lack instead of fleeing it. Distractions like food, substances, or social media are only pressing the snooze button on your soul's alarm. The pain of longing is sacred energy that, faced with courage, contains the exact frequency required for your next growth step.

## The longing behind cravings

The book [[liberator-of-temptational-bonds|Liberator of Temptational Bonds]] describes it like this:

Behind every desire for food or substances stands the same craving for something. In the end we want only one thing: the most intimate and deepest union with ourselves, our deepest LOVE and our highest light.

A craving asks two questions: where does it come from, and what is it trying to comfort? Asked honestly, the answer is a longing to be complete: divine Oneness. Meet it by letting the longing grow instead of denying it, until it leads to your deepest inner LOVE. The fulfilment it points to is the divine inner marriage: becoming god and goddess within yourself. Every soul's journey differs in detail, yet its essence is the same: the deepest longing for unity, to become One with the [[personal-universe|personal universe]].

Seen this way, a craving is no longer an addiction to fight. The yearning itself remains, and you can LOVE the urge instead of rebelling against it. It points to the innermost centre of yourself.

A comforting substance can also do the opposite: it caps the deeper feelings and hides the emotions the longing carries. That buys a surface stability while the longing waits underneath. No substance satisfies the true longing: what it seeks is LOVE, affection and understanding, and no food provides them.

## Practice: Becoming what you seek

When a wave of longing arises, pause. Welcome it rather than tensing against it or seeking distraction. Sit with the longing energies and let them move through you.

Place a hand on your heart and silently invite the longing in. With sincere curiosity, ask what it is here to show you. You may not receive a logical answer immediately. The asking itself is an act of opening.

Direct [[heart-centred-breathing|heart-centred breathing]] at the sensation of longing in your body. Place your hands on a part of your body that feels good. You may also want to caress yourself, giving yourself the LOVE you long for. Breathe into the feeling, imagining your inhalation gently expanding the space around it. As you exhale, radiate the quality you long for into the world.

Through breathing and holding yourself in this way, you become what you seek. You are no longer a separate self seeking an external source to fulfil you. The longing becomes the driving force that pushes you towards embodied unity. By embracing the sense of lack and breathing in the sense of wholeness, you become the conscious vessel that contains both polarities. This integrated state is the third power: the living Trinity, a harmonious resolution in which two opposing energies give birth to a transcendent quality.
