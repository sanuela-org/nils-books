# Inner Sanctuary

## Definition

An inner sanctuary is a place of safety that is generated within. It is a portable sense of comfort, belonging and warmth that you carry into every situation instead of building walls. The German word *Geborgenheit* captures it: the feeling of being perfectly held, of deep emotional safety and nourishment. Unlike a fortress, which offers rigid safety built on alertness and resistance, the sanctuary provides warm, flexible safety rooted in acceptance and nurture. The heart centre is the source of this inner home, generating the warmth and light that radiate outward. The practices below generate the sanctuary on demand.

## Practices

### Practice: Heart-centred breathing

This is the master key to the inner sanctuary. It instantly pulls your awareness out of your mind and into the calm, intuitive space of your heart. It can be done anywhere, in any moment. Simply breathe LOVE in and out through the centre of your chest as though your heart itself were breathing.

The same breathing technique supports clear, compassionate communication. For its use in dialogue, see [[heart-centred-communication|heart-centred communication]].

### Practice: The soothing circuit

This practice settles the nervous system through physical touch.

- Place one hand over the heart centre on the chest.
- Place the other hand on a spot on the stomach that needs holding — wherever the body signals a need for comfort.
- Breathe normally and let the nervous system settle. There is no need to visualise or do anything. Just feel the connection.
- To soften the armour around the heart further, caress your chest in slow, small circles, as if comforting a child.

### Practice: Physical anchors of comfort

The body responds to physical signals of safety, even simple ones. Hug a pillow to your chest, or wrap yourself snugly in a blanket to create a cocoon of *Geborgenheit*. The body responds to what it receives. Use these signals when the sanctuary feels hard to generate from within, as a bridge back to the heart centre.

## A portable home

The inner sanctuary travels with you. Difficult conversations and stressful meetings become environments where you can breathe consciously through your heart centre and reconnect with the warmth within. You do not need to leave a situation to find safety. You carry it.

From this inner stability, community becomes a natural extension. Shared acceptance, longer hugs, attentive listening, these flow naturally from someone rooted in their own *Geborgenheit*. You don't need to join a community based on what you lack; you can join to share the sanctuary you've already cultivated.

From this inner stability, friendliness radiates outward and the world mirrors it back. The more safety you generate within, the more the world reflects it in kinder interactions, unexpected support, and an unshakable sense of being held. See [[friendly-universe|friendly universe]].
