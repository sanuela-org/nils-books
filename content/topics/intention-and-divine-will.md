# Intention and Divine Will

## Definition

Intention and divine will is the sacred surrender at the heart of all true [[magick|magick]]: aligning our personal will with the higher divine plan. Placing human desires on the altar does not negate them. It allows them to be refined and guided by a greater wisdom than our own. This alignment is not passive resignation but active manifestation. We embrace our deepest yearnings, then release our attachment to how they must manifest with LOVE, stating our preferences and taking aligned action while trusting divine intelligence with the details, timing and form.

## Lucifer's mistake and Jesus' prayer

The book [[kind-of-magick|A Kind of Magick]] describes it like this:

Two archetypes hold the contrast. Tradition tells that the Fall arose from the refusal to surrender: the insistence of a separate will placing itself above the divine harmony. This is the ultimate root of egocentric magick, the ego claiming sovereignty over the cosmos. Its opposite is the prayer of Jesus, honest about his human desire in his time of greatest distress, then the ultimate act of sacred surrender. He showed that true power lies not in getting what we want, but in choosing what is best for everyone.

To pray for divine will to be done is therefore not an act of weakness but one of immense courage and trust. Our limited perspective cannot grasp the full complexity of cause and effect, learning and soul evolution that the divine will encompasses.

## Asking to receive what we already feel

Approaching prayer and magick from a place of lack reinforces the state of not having. Pleading and desperation serve only to strengthen the very condition we wish to change. True manifesting power arises from trust and a felt sense of inner abundance.

The instruction is to believe you have already received: to feel the gratitude, peace and joy of the wish fulfilled now, in this very moment. Doubt is a frequency that disrupts alignment with the reality we wish to attract. Ask according to divine will, trusting that you receive what your soul truly needs to grow, even when this does not align with what you seem to want.

Focus on the core feeling of the desired outcome, whether that be safety, LOVE, vitality or freedom, and entrust the how. Manifesting tunes your energies to the frequency of a parallel reality where this is already your truth. Deeper motives behind any request belong to [[intention-behind-the-intention|Intention Behind the Intention]].

## The Permaculture Principle in Magick

The core idea of the permaculture principle: any single action should yield multiple beneficial outcomes. In gardening, a tree provides fruit, offers shade for other plants, creates a wind shield, stabilises the soil with its roots, and creates a habitat for insects and birds.

Planting rose bushes to beautify your garden can at the same time be a ritual act to attract more LOVE and compassion into your personal universe, or to symbolically create a protective boundary of thorns. A single, conscious action is done for multi-layered reasons.

Craft your intentions and rituals so that one act fulfils more than one purpose. See the bigger picture, not only your personal goal: how can your magick contribute to the healing or growth of the collective or your community, and how can it nourish the energetic grid of your neighbourhood? This approach creates a web of interconnected, supportive energies, making your magick more efficient, harmonious and deeply rooted in a larger field, like a healthy ecosystem.

## The soul's curriculum

Each soul walks a unique journey. The progression of stages, as [[kind-of-magick|A Kind of Magick]] lays them out:

- Stage 1: learning to trust that manifestation works through small, personal desires.
- Stage 2: realising that self-centred goals lose their magnetism and energy flows when intention links to a greater sense of purpose.
- Stage 3: realising the seamless unity of the personal and the divine, where our abundance naturally becomes a blessing to the world around us.

The divine plan is not concerned with what is being manifested, but with the why: the quality being learned and embodied through the act of creation itself.

## Living the alignment

Liberated creators do not become lonely gods in empty space, acting from an isolated will. Your [[personal-universe|personal universe]] is always part of the greater whole. Through continuous growth, we can become partners of [[gaia|Gaia]]; we evolve from following her curriculum to consciously collaborating with it.

The question shifts from "Is this allowed?" to "Does this create more LOVE, beauty, and truth in the world?". The feeling in the heart is the compass. An intention coming from divine LOVE, compassion and a genuine wish to contribute to the greater good co-creates with divine will. Creation from ego, control or separation gives a subtle dissonance, a tightening or a slight unease, as feedback from our natural connection to the divine. This is the conscious balance: using our power with the humility to listen to the harmonious chorus of the whole.
