# Intention Behind the Intention

## Definition

The intention behind the intention is the honest self-inquiry that peels a surface desire back to the soul's essence underneath. A wish for money may actually be a longing for security or freedom. The desire for a beautiful house may be the soul's call for a safe space of peace or creative expression. To practise [[magick|magick]] with integrity, we must peel back these layers and move from the ego's wants to the soul's essence. The goal is the authentic intention emerging from within, not a substitute that fills an inner void.

## From desire to essence

The book [[kind-of-magick|A Kind of Magick]] describes it like this:

One possible way forward is to ask a series of mirror questions.

1. **Acknowledge the desire.** Ask yourself: What is my strongest desire right now? Write the answer down as it comes to you. This is the seed.
2. **Explore its source.** Ask: Why do I want this? Write down the first answer that comes to mind, then ask again and go deeper with each layer: What will this give me? How will I feel once I have it? What will it enable me to do? You are searching for the root.
3. **Distinguish the symbol from its meaning.** You may realise that the object itself is not the goal, but the feeling it represents. Ask: If I already had this, what core feeling would remain? Are there other ways I can experience this quality right now? You are seeking the core.
4. **Test the resonance.** Your soul will always guide you towards expansion, lightness and greater LOVE, whereas your ego will often lead you towards contraction, fear or a need for control. Consider: When I think of this desire, do I breathe more deeply and feel more relaxed? Does this desire make me want to share and radiate, or to keep it just for myself?
5. **Connect your essence to the divine whole.** Recognise your natural radiance. Ask: When I embody this feeling, how will it naturally benefit others? How will this intention contribute to the growth of my soul and the world around me? You are building a bridge.
6. **Anchor the intention.** Gather your reflections and anchor them in your heart. Shape them into a single, clear declaration that unites the symbol and the essence. Ask: What is the purest form of this intention? How can I express it in one sentence that feels completely authentic?

An example of the journey:

- Desire: "I want €10,000."
- Why? "To feel safe."
- Essence: "I want to experience deep trust and peace."
- Resonance: "This feeling relaxes me and allows me to breathe fully."
- Bridge: "When I feel trust and peace, I can create a stable, joyful space to share with others."
- Final intention: "I welcome unwavering trust and deep peace, which allow me to create an abundant life that nourishes and supports me and others."

It is important to view money not as the ultimate goal, but as a potential means towards the life experiences our soul craves: safety, freedom, growth or adventure. By guiding and maintaining our focus, we enable Gaia's intelligence to determine the optimal means. The desired sum may arrive, or an even better solution may present itself. We trust [[gaia|Gaia]] or [[personal-universe|our personal universe]] to provide the experience in the form that best serves our growth.

Why some souls manifest simple desires with ease while others must attach a higher purpose to succeed is the soul's curriculum — see [[intention-and-divine-will|Intention and Divine Will]].

## The dialogue of manifestation

When we ask for qualities such as freedom, trust or safety, it is important to understand how Gaia responds. She does not usually give us a gift wrapped up and ready to open. She presents us with opportunities to embody that quality.

Asking for 'trust' may not provide a tangible guarantee. It can lead us into situations that require us to find and practise this deep trust within ourselves. Gaia, or the personal universe, acts as a wise coach: if you call for 'safety', situations may arise that show you how to keep yourself safe. If you ask for 'freedom', you will encounter moments that require the courage to prioritise yourself.

Your [[nine-step-manifesting-ritual|magickal ritual]] is never a waste of energy. It is a powerful mirror. If you ask for something, such as money, and feel only emptiness, that indicates you need to explore your intention further. If you ask and experience genuine joy and expansion, you are probably on the right path.

Think of this process like tending a garden. Your intention is the seed. Gaia provides the soil and conditions. If you ask for money but what your soul truly needs is security and joy, she may provide something else that carries the same essential qualities. Your garden will always respond; it just might not grow exactly as you first imagined.

With this understanding, there is no need to be afraid to ask. Manifestation is not like ordering from a catalogue. It is a loving dialogue with life, designed to help us become the kind of humans who naturally embody the qualities we seek. The desired state is already within us, waiting to be awakened and realised.

## The five-phase practice

This entire process can be embodied through a simple, accessible practice. Rather than a rigid formula, consider it a guide for creative expression through meditation, prayer, dance, intuitive rituals or writing.

1. **Desire (the seed).** Hold your initial desire without judgement. Speak or write it plainly, as if you are holding a seed in your hand.
2. **Essence (the soil).** Ask yourself what this desire truly means to you. What are you really longing for? Does fulfilling this desire help you to grow? This is the nutrient-rich soil where you plant your seed.
3. **Invitation (the water).** Gently invite this quality into your life, with words or a gesture, but avoid dictating the exact form. This is the water that nourishes the seed. Example: "I invite freedom and safety, which allow me to create a space full of joy and abundance and the ability to create what I need for my growth."
4. **Embodiment (the sun).** Choose a small, daily action that allows you to experience this quality in your body. This is the sunlight that helps the seed to grow. It could involve breathing deeply to embody trust, lighting a candle to embody clarity, or moving with a sense of freedom. Example: drink your morning tea consciously, as if you already live in freedom and abundance.
5. **Openness to form (space).** Complete your practice by letting go of how it will manifest. State your openness to receiving this essence in any form, and trust that Gaia will deliver it in a way even better than you could have imagined. This creates a space for miracles to flourish. Example: "I am open to experiencing this freedom and safety in any form, even better than I can currently imagine."

This framework turns manifestation into a creative dialogue: a conscious cultivation of the soul that connects the earthly and the divine, transforming wishful thinking into collaborative power.
