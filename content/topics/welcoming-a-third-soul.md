# Welcoming a Third Soul

## Definition

A trusted third soul is a person a couple welcomes into their bond to give rather than take. Anyone getting close from the outside is often seen as a threat. When another soul is invited in to give, that closeness becomes a space without competition or rivalry, where more trust, deeper honesty and greater emotional growth are possible.

## What the supporter does

In most traditional relationships, when challenges arise, couples usually have to seek help from a professional outside their relationship. But what if there was already someone close who could offer support and presence before the problems became too big? A third soul, trusted and caring, can bring in fresh energies, new perspectives, and emotional grounding and balance to both partners.

It takes care, openness, and clear communication to make it work – but the rewards can be rich and beautiful.

Having a third soul close to a couple can bring new inspiration, balance and support – but this only works when they have the right attitude and emotional maturity. Being a true supporter is not about stepping into others' emotions or trying to 'fix' anything. It is about being present with an open heart, offering a safe and steady space.

A supportive third soul stays calm, takes genuine joy in the other two's happiness, holds space without control, and communicates honestly. They honour boundaries, wait without pushing, need no attention, and stay centred in emotional waves. 

The third soul needs to bring a simple set of qualities and attitudes with them to strengthen the existing bond between two souls rather than unintentionally creating new tensions.

## Benefits

The book [[soulfamilies|Soulfamilies]] describes it like this:

- Brings fresh inspiration and new energy into the relationship.
- Helps prevent emotional stagnation and boredom.
- Offers steady emotional support when one or both partners go through difficulties.
- Encourages more honesty, openness, and the courage to be vulnerable.
- Helps partners break out of old, ingrained communication patterns.
- Can create a stronger feeling of safety and connection for everyone involved.
- Reduces pressure on the couple to be "everything" for each other.
- Allows old patterns of mistrust or jealousy to heal through shared experience.
- Encourages setting allowances and healthy boundaries and respecting personal space.
- Opens the way for deeper emotional growth and true forgiveness.
- Helps overcome feelings of neglect, loneliness, or unfulfillment.
- Encourages emotional healing and more honest, heart-based connections.
- Allows deeper, closer friendships to develop alongside the relationship.
- Strengthens the experience of unconditional LOVE, acceptance and surrender.

## Checklist for a Supportive Third Soul

- Brings calmness and balance into the connection.
- Feels genuine joy when the other two are happy – from an open heart.
- Holds space with kindness and steadiness, without needing to control.
- Communicates honestly, clearly and respectfully.
- Understands and honours personal allowances and emotional boundaries.
- Is patient and not pushing for things to happen.
- Is comfortable with closeness but also with giving space when needed.
- Does not seek attention or validation, but naturally radiates trust and grounded presence.
- Stays centred during emotional waves and does not take sides.
- Feels deep empathy and shows emotional maturity.
- Can communicate personal feelings without blame, guilt or accusation.
- Brings inspiration and new perspectives without judgement.
- Values and protects the trust given by the others.
- Understands that LOVE grows best in freedom, not through ownership or demand.
