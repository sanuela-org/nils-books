# Magickal Forgiveness

## Definition

Magickal forgiveness is the conscious, heart-centred act of freeing yourself from the emotional ties of a painful story. Before you can build something new with a clear intention, you must first clear the energy of the past from your field. Resentment, anger and hurt act like static on a radio frequency: they distort your signal and prevent your [[magick|magick]] from taking effect. Probably the most effective way to eliminate this interference is through conscious, heart-centred forgiveness. It is an internal, private act, performed in the silent theatre of your own heart and imagination — not just with words.

## The Ho'oponopono story

The book [[kind-of-magick|A Kind of Magick]] describes it like this:

The therapist Dr. Hew Len practised Ho'oponopono, a Hawaiian tradition of forgiveness. He healed an entire unit of violent criminals in a Hawaiian hospital without ever meeting the patients in person. Instead, he repeatedly reviewed their files and practised forgiveness, saying to himself and to the energy he held about them: "I'm sorry, please forgive me, thank you, I LOVE you." Within months, the once chaotic unit became calm, and eventually it was closed.

This is the real power of forgiveness. When we transform our inner energies, the outside world has no choice but to reflect that change. Forgiveness does not just change you; it changes the very fabric of the reality around you.

## The four-step forgiveness ritual

**Step 1: Centre yourself.** Begin with [[heart-centred-breathing|heart-centred breathing]]. Place a hand on your chest and breathe LOVE in and out until you feel calm and present.

**Step 2: Invoke the connection.** In your mind's eye, bring forward the image of the soul you feel hurt by. See them standing before you. Notice any resistance or emotion that arises, and breathe LOVE into it. Is there anything you would like to share with them or ask them?

**Step 3: Offer an embrace.** Imagine taking a step forward and embracing this soul with a warm, compassionate hug. You may not agree with what they did, but you can still acknowledge the wounded soul behind it. Like all of us, they are struggling and may not know how to do their own inner work.

As you hold them in this imagined embrace, consider repeating these words in your heart:

- "I forgive you for the part you played in my story."
- "I release you and myself from this energy."

Imagine any lingering negative energies between you dissolving into light as you hold them.

**Step 4: Close with gratitude.** Imagine the hug ending peacefully. See them turn and walk away, surrounded by light. Bring your awareness back to your own heart and feel yourself becoming clearer, lighter and more at peace.

## Forgiving ourselves

We often need our own forgiveness more than anyone else's. To move forward, we need to release not only the hurts we have received, but also the roles we have played, including that of the victim, as well as the hurts we have caused others. When you forgive yourself, you break the cycle of karma and reclaim your full creative power.

Use the same heart-centred practice, gently shifting the focus towards yourself. After becoming centred with heart breathing, picture a past version of yourself — someone who made a mistake, acted from a place of pain or felt trapped in a victim role. See your former self clearly, with compassion and without criticism.

Imagine your present, wiser self entering this memory. You are not there to judge the past, but to bring understanding and LOVE to this moment. Open your arms and embrace your younger self with deep compassion. Give support to the self who did their best with the knowledge they had available. Feel the pain, shame or regret begin to soften and dissolve in the warmth of your acceptance.

You could whisper these words to your heart:

- "I forgive you for not knowing better."
- "I forgive you for the role you played."
- "I release us both from this story."
- "You are worthy of my LOVE, exactly as you are."

Feel the two parts of yourself — past and present — merging back into one integrated whole. Breathe deeply and allow the peace of this reconciliation to fill every cell of your body. This is the final step of the ritual: embracing all aspects of yourself and welcoming them home.
