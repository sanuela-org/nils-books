# Moon Love Relationship

## Definition

A Moon Love relationship is a bond between two close friends who come together in a more sensual way once per lunar cycle: a sacred, intimate LOVE ceremony, with personal allowances and boundaries agreed for the time between. A committed, loving freedom — close connection without the demand of a full relationship.

## Across books

Named in [[soulfamilies|Soulfamilies]] among the new possibilities beyond the couple model.

[[liberator-of-temptational-bonds|Liberator of Temptational Bonds]] works the model out in more detail: two close friends keep a sphere of light of almost unconditional LOVE around their bond, unconditional but for one agreed condition — reduced intimacy between their meetings.
