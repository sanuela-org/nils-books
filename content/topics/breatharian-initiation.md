# Breatharian Initiation

## Definition

After the breatharian initiation, eating becomes more a way of achieving energetic grounding and emotional balance while becoming more conscious, aware, and awake without food. Living more and more on astral nourishment means drawing energy directly from the astral realms instead of from food. This may involve eating only once a day, once a week, or even less frequently. 

## The process

The transition to astral nourishment is not a sudden stop in eating but a gradual process. Food intake is reduced over the first two days, and on the third day a green smoothie in the morning can still provide the body with nutrients. Then a six-day fast begins. Initially, energy may feel lower, but a breakthrough usually occurs, often resulting in more energy than before.

The key mechanism is the chakras, which connect the astral body to the physical body. When the astral body "goes food shopping" in the astral realms, it can obtain everything it needs for free. The breath is only part of this process, which is why the term "breatharian" is not fully accurate. Germans call the process _Lichtnahrungsprozess_, though it has nothing to do with sunlight — rather, it involves higher frequencies. 

For the body to learn this, it must "jump into the cold water once and learn how to swim in the astral realms." A crucial requirement is feeling physically and emotionally safe throughout, so the mind and brain can learn new pathways. It will not happen if one believes they are starving. Instead, one seeks the benefits of fasting and connecting with fine, subtle energies for a few days, watching as the body intuitively finds a way to make it work.

## Integration

The process is intuitive and transformative, and one can freely choose how to take the first steps. Full integration typically takes many months. During this time, one balances and finds their best personal way of living with or without food, often going through different phases.
