# The Healer & Creator Technique

## Definition

Healer & Creator is a fusion of energetic healing technique and [[magick|magick]], designed to bridge the gap between intention and embodiment. Its core practice is a simple, flowing movement that gathers and circulates your personal life force energy, chi. You guide it from the earth through your body upwards into your expanded energy field. Once this energy is awakened and flowing, you learn to shape it. You can use it to cleanse yourself and your surroundings, to infuse yourself with specific qualities such as courage or LOVE, or to support others. The technique gives a direct, physical experience of energy and builds a high-vibrational charge within your astral body. It empowers you to not just think about a new reality, but to become it energetically, fundamentally shifting the frequency you radiate out into your world.

## The energy flow exercise

The book [[kind-of-magick|A Kind of Magick]] describes it like this:

An instruction video shows the exact procedure slowly and in detail: [Energy Flow video](https://www.healer-and-creator.de/en/energyflow). Watch it several times to internalise the energy cycle; do the exercise along with the video first, and then without any help.

For the exercise you need a bit of space around you, so that you can stretch your arms in all directions without touching anything.

Stand straight, relaxed and steady. Your feet are about a foot apart. Your knees are not completely pushed through, but a little bit buckled, so that your stand is slightly springy. Your arms hang relaxed on both sides. The palms are held open at the top and slightly arched, in a receiving posture, as if you were forming two small bowls.

Start your exercise with an inner smile and [[heart-centred-breathing|breathe through your heart centre]] a few times.

With the next inhalation through the nose, bring both hands inward to the middle of the body until they almost touch each other. Continue to inhale, pulling both hands slowly and evenly upwards. Imagine how you pull up the life energy in the body. Keep breathing and keep your hands moving up until your arms are stretched all the way up; your palms should have opened slightly on the way.

Then, just as evenly and slowly, start exhaling through the slightly opened mouth as you guide your arms down in a circle, palms directed towards the body. Imagine how you create a large sphere of energy and light around your body with your hands. When you have exhaled completely, your hands have reached the bottom.

Without a pause, immediately start breathing in through the nose again. Turn your palms upwards into the receiving posture, bring your hands to the middle, and without a break, slowly back up. As you breathe slowly through your nose, you draw the life force upward with the symbolic help of your hands, as if you were pulling up a weight that hangs from your hands with a rope. Open the palms at the top, and while exhaling, bring your hands down sideways, forming a sphere of energy and light around your body.

It is a perfect circuit that you create with inhalation, exhalation, and your hands and arms.

The inhalation and exhalation should be done in a relaxed way. Although you consciously control the breathing rhythm, the breath should not be pushed. At the beginning, repeat the exercise five to ten times, with no break in between. You can keep your eyes open or close them, whichever helps you focus on the flow of breath and energy. Make your movements as round, flowing and harmonious as possible. The better you can concentrate and use your imagination — first pulling the energy up, then spreading it around the body, and down again, to find your centre and base for the next energy cycle — the more energy you will feel flowing through your hands and your body.

Do not overdo it in the beginning. Make sure it stays comfortable; if you feel a slight tension in your head, take a little break. The exercise needs to be understood and internalised, but it can also give you so much new life energy, or chi, that you might have to learn to handle it first.

Ten passes in the morning and ten more in the afternoon or evening is a good measure. If necessary, you can increase the number of passes slowly after a few weeks, or do ten passes three or four times a day. If you practise this energy technique regularly, you will probably notice a significant change in your life after just a few days.

## Energy spheres

After practising the Energy Flow Exercise for ten or more passes, you have channelled the chi around you. You have increased the flow of energy through your body and strengthened your own energy field. Now you are ready for the second part, which is always practised right after the Energy Flow Exercise. In it you learn to send out energy spheres and use them for your personal purposes.

A second video shows this part of the technique: [Sending energy video](https://www.healer-and-creator.de/en/sendingenergy).

To form the spheres, imagine how you would hold a ball in front of your body. Hold your hands in front of you, the fingers slightly curved inward to encircle the shape of the ball. Try to feel the round shape of the ball on your palms, and visualise that you are holding a ball of life energy.

Two little movements help you experience this as intensively as possible:

1. Gently press your hands together, as if you were squeezing the ball a little bit. Then move both palms a little bit apart from each other, only for two or three centimetres, and bring them back a little closer again, as if you were holding the ball tighter once more. The slight pulling and pushing should help you feel a direct feedback in the form of an invisible resistance on your palms.
2. Turn your palms alternately with contrary movements of your wrists. With sensitive perception, every little twist may let you feel the shell of the energy sphere more or less clearly. It is bundled Universal Life Energy that you can sense here.

Combine the two movements: pull your palms a few centimetres apart while twisting your wrists opposite to each other, then push them a few centimetres further together while again twisting them in opposite directions. Try to feel this luminous energy sphere between your hands as clearly and intensely as possible. If you do not feel anything, just keep practising and continue to imagine that there really is a ball in your hands.

Once you have a good perception of your energy sphere, you can send it towards its destination, which depends on the application you want to do. Imagine that the energy sphere can float in the air. Slowly open your hands and push the ball of energy in a gentle motion in the direction of its target. For the first round of practice, you can target any item in your field of view — it does not matter whether you send your sphere to a picture on the wall or to a car parked in front of you. Just send out your energy and get a sense of how it slowly drifts in the direction you are pushing it.

Immediately afterwards, create a new energy sphere with your hands, and after a few seconds, you can send it out in the direction of your target. With a little practice, shaping a new sphere takes only about three seconds. Then you send it out and form the next one. Repeat this process about five to ten times.

## Self-healing and harmonisation

H&C is a very universal healing and energy technique. Before you can start sending energetic healing energy to other people, first learn how to harmonise, strengthen and heal your own energy field — your aura.

All H&C applications follow the same basic procedure. First, you always do some rounds of the Energy Flow Exercise: about ten passes of inhaling through the nose, pulling the energy up, and exhaling through the mouth, forming an energy circle around you. Then you shape the energy spheres with the coordinating movements of your hands and send them out, about five to ten passes. For the various applications, you slightly vary a few details or concentrate on sending the spheres to a specific target.

For self-healing, begin with the inner smile and breathe through your heart centre. Practise the flow, and each time you spread your arms above you and lower them in a circle with the exhale, let the energy you have just drawn up trickle down on you like in a shower. Treat yourself to this wonderful energy shower in every round, and imagine intensively how the concentrated energy from your hands trickles down on you. If you want, you can say "I LOVE me" or "I'm wonderful!" while exhaling and enjoying it. Practise the flow exercise with the energy shower at least ten times.

Then start shaping and sending out the spheres, directing them onto your own body. You can send one sphere into your left leg, then one into your right leg, then into your abdominal region, your heart centre, and finally dip your face and head into a sphere. You can also send your spheres preferentially to those parts of your body that you may not like about yourself, or to regions where you have a physical problem or can use fresh life energy for some other reason.

Do yourself good with this application. When you are in harmony with your body and your energy field, you will soon be able to heal other people energetically as well.

## Making H&C a habit

Daily practice is the one thing that matters before you learn more applications. If you only use H&C once or twice a week, you will not nearly achieve what you can with this technique. Through daily practice you strengthen your inner energy channels, and step by step you get an ever finer sense of the flow of energy within and around you. Over time you will reach deeper levels and become acquainted with new aspects of the technique that may not be apparent now. Internal blockages will often resolve themselves. Many report a better sleep, because H&C is stress-reducing, and find that they experience everyday life much more consciously and that smaller crises cannot pull them down as much as they used to.

It only takes five minutes for the Energy Flow and Energy Sphere Exercises. You will actually regain these minutes during the day, as H&C will make you feel more balanced. The only way to experience the long-term effects is to practise each day for two or three weeks. If you still find it a waste of time after that, you can easily end your new habit.

Once you have decided to make H&C an integral part of your daily life, you need a concrete plan for integrating the exercises into your everyday life. The simplest trick is to insert the new action between two regular habits. Suppose you always brush your teeth as the last action of your morning routine, and immediately after that you prepare yourself a tea. Insert the five minutes for H&C right after brushing your teeth and before drinking the tea, and it will become a regular habit after just a few days. Your new exercise is supported by the two habits before and after it. Some say a new habit takes 21 days to establish; with this simple trick it can work in just one week.

You will have a much deeper experience if you practise at least twice a day. For most people it is not a good idea to practise right before bedtime: with so much energy, many cannot fall asleep easily. The best time for most is between lunch and dinner. Find a daily habit that is already established during this period — the meal itself, arriving home from work, or your evening relaxation — and anchor the exercises to it. Those who already meditate or do yoga, tai chi or similar exercises have it even easier: just do the H&C exercises directly before or directly afterwards. Before, you will probably find your other exercises gain a much better concentration and depth; after, the H&C exercises themselves become more focused and profound.

During your day, the inner smile and the breathing through your heart centre are ideal pick-me-up techniques that connect to your H&C practice. Many users do both regularly in between — while queuing, while driving, or during other activities that run more or less on autopilot. Practising outdoors or in front of an open balcony door can make the effect more intense.

## Embodying specific qualities

Once you have learned the basic techniques for balancing and strengthening your energy field and established a regular practice, you can consciously integrate specific energetic qualities from various sources into your personal energy field. This enhances your connection to higher frequencies and aligns your personal universe with your deepest intentions.

Start with the inner smile and breathe several times through your heart centre. This grounds you in divine LOVE and prepares you to receive and integrate powerful energies. During the upward movement of the Energy Flow Exercise, focus your intention on a specific energetic quality that you wish to integrate:

- To embody nurturing, steadfast LOVE, imagine calling upon the Mother archetype. Feel her essence of unconditional care, stability and fertile grounding rising within you from the depths of Mother Earth.
- To embody transformation and fierce courage, connect with the energy of Fire. Imagine drawing its passionate intensity, purifying clarity and powerful will up from your core.
- Invite Archangel Michael when you are seeking protection and strength. Feel his courageous energy, clear boundaries and liberation from fear rising through your being.
- To amplify unconditional LOVE and emotional healing, focus on rose quartz. Imagine its gentle yet powerful energy flowing upwards and filling you with compassion for yourself and others.

As you raise your hands above your head and then lower them in a circular motion around your body as you exhale, imagine these specific energies showering over you, filling every cell of your body and your energetic sphere and becoming an active and vibrant part of your being. As you integrate them, internally affirm your gratitude to the energy you have tuned yourself to: "Thank you for your support. I lovingly integrate you into my being!" Visualise your personal energetic sphere glowing brightly with the newly integrated qualities, as vivid, colourful and strong as possible. Repeat this modified Energy Flow Exercise at least ten times.

Then proceed to form and send out your energy spheres, infusing them with the amplified qualities. You could send spheres filled with the nurturing energy of the Mother to support a family relationship, or spheres imbued with the courage of Fire to empower a new creative project. Send them gently and lovingly to your chosen destination: into your immediate surroundings, towards a specific goal, or — with consent — to another person for their highest good. Repeat at least five times.

The same flow exercise can transmute what weighs you down. A dense feeling such as judgement, drawn up consciously with the inhale and integrated with the exhale, can soften into the gentler light of rose quartz and fuel a higher frequency.

## More H&C applications

The true power of the technique lies in its versatility. It provides a framework you can adapt to consciously channel energies towards any area of your personal life. The following applications are suggestions to inspire your own intuition; feel free to customise the technique for your own journey. Your intuition is, and always will be, your most important guide. Begin each application with the inner smile and several breaths through your heart centre.

**Expanding your energetic field.** As you exhale and lower your arms in a circular motion, visualise the channelled life energy not only trickling onto your body, but also expanding several metres around you in all directions: forwards, backwards, left, right, upwards and downwards. Imagine filling this expanded field with LOVE and light, and repeat the exercise at least ten times. Then send your energy spheres outwards in all directions, programming them to remain active around you all day, continuously enriching your expanded field.

**Cleansing and nourishing your space.** Open all room and large cabinet doors, and, if you are comfortable with it, the windows to allow a light breeze in. Stand in the centre of the room, or an intuitive symbolic centre. As you exhale and lower your arms in a circle, imagine your arms becoming very long and encompassing the entire room, filling it with loving light as you sweep them around it. Then send energy spheres into each corner of the room, visualising them gently washing away any negative energy that has accumulated there. You may also want to send spheres to the furniture. Then move to the next room and repeat the process.

**Charging a talisman.** Choose an object of personal significance and cleanse it energetically first, for example by taking it in your hand and imagining how old energies dissolve as you blow on it. Decide on one specific quality you wish to infuse into the object, such as more LOVE, heightened awareness or openness to opportunities. Place the object in your line of sight, about an arm's length away. As you exhale, visualise having already achieved your desired outcome — for example experiencing intense LOVE in your heart — and form a large energy sphere with this visualisation of intense emotion directly in front of your body, strengthening it with each subsequent exhalation, about ten times. Then form spheres with the energies you have just created and gently send them onto your chosen talisman, visualising it absorbing and storing them. Repeat this process at least five times.

**Healing Gaia.** Focus on a specific healing theme for the Earth, such as 'Earth's Ecosystem', 'Peaceful Coexistence' or 'More Love Among People'. As you exhale and lower your arms in a circular motion, visualise a scaled-down version of the Earth floating between your hands. Gently stroke the imaginary aura of Gaia, filling it with light and your chosen healing intention, and visualise your intention as already being a reality within this image of the Earth. Then create energy spheres, fill them with your chosen healing intention and send them out in all directions: forward, right, backward, left, up and down.

**Cultivating healthy eating habits.** As you inhale, deeply feel the joyful anticipation of your body's cells as you mentally commit to gradually reducing your appetite for unhealthy foods. Promise your cells that you will increasingly choose high-quality nutrition to provide them with the nutrients they need, prioritising their well-being. As you exhale, integrate this inner promise into your energy body. Then form energy spheres filled with the intention of joyful, healthy nourishment, and send them into both your stomach and your kitchen.

**Embracing your shadow sides.** Before starting the flow exercise, take a moment to acknowledge a shadow aspect of yourself that you recognise. Allow yourself to feel it without analysing or judging it; trusting your intuition is key here, and it is perfectly fine if you cannot describe what you feel. As you inhale and draw your hands and energies upwards from your centre, consciously draw the energies of your shadow aspects upwards too. As you exhale and lower your arms in a circle, visualise integrating these energies into your inner being. You may wish to affirm internally: "I am okay. I LOVE all parts of me!" Repeat at least ten times. After the flow, the energies you have circulated are purified of their perceived negative qualities and serve as a powerful foundation for your energy spheres. Form them according to your desires and send them as little angelic helpers to the areas of your life that need the most healing, or direct them towards your current projects and goals.
