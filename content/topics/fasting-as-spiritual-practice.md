# Fasting as Spiritual Practice

## Definition

The purifying power of fasting is a tool that can provide many physical and spiritual benefits when practised in a way that feels good and right for one's own body.

## What fasting does

The book [[liberator-of-temptational-bonds|Liberator of Temptational Bonds]] describes it as the courageous act of turning inwards. By stepping back from the constant cycle of consumption, you give yourself the opportunity to connect with a deeper, more authentic part of yourself, step by step opening to a more heightened state of superconsciousness.

Fasting removes the mental fog that can come from overindulgence in food or substances. More importantly, it makes you more receptive to the moving energies of your body, the world and the greater consciousness around you — like tuning a radio to a clearer frequency. It raises the astral frequencies, brings new clarity and helps to break the remaining links your physical form has with the [[thorns-of-craving|thorns of craving]].

Like holding a key to a treasure chest of spiritual gifts, we can feel the astral tingling more and can better enjoy the energy exchange during the [[hugging-meditations|hugging meditations]].

## How it is practised

The [[guided-meditations-for-cravings|Embracing the Power of Fasting]] meditation can be used as a helpful preparation for it.

The entry for a longer fasting period can be done gradually: reduce your food intake over three days, then begin to fast, experiencing it as a conscious affirmation of your spiritual essence. Everything happens in phases, as it feels good and right for you and your body. 

How about turning the practice into a game challenge: fast one full day, then feast until fully satiated, then fast again for at least two days — one day longer each round, to feel the difference between the fasting and the feasting days.
