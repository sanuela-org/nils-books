# Heart-centred Breathing

## Definition

Heart-centred breathing is a foundational practice: you imagine breathing with conscious awareness through the heart centre instead of through the nose. It instantly pulls your awareness out of your mind and into the calm, intuitive space of your heart. It can be done anywhere, in any moment. It is the master key to creating the [[inner-sanctuary|inner sanctuary]]. Many other practices build on it, from [[heart-centred-communication|heart-centred communication]] to the [[nine-step-manifesting-ritual|Nine-Step Manifesting Ritual]].

## The practice

Breathe naturally, but imagine that the air is flowing through your heart centre instead of through your nose. You can place your hand in the centre of your chest if you want to feel it more strongly: LOVE in, LOVE out. You don't need to control anything, just let the breath flow. Feel and breathe.

### The golden stream

In [[kind-of-magick|A Kind of Magick]], the breath carries a golden stream of LOVE: with each inhalation, draw in pure, golden LOVE through the heart centre; with each exhalation, feel that LOVE radiate through your entire being and into the surrounding space. This golden stream is the opening of every ritual, the [[healer-creator-technique|Healer & Creator Technique]], [[magickal-forgiveness|magickal forgiveness]] and Step 1 of the [[nine-step-manifesting-ritual|Nine-Step Manifesting Ritual]]. Maintain it throughout: it keeps your energy grounded in divine LOVE, protecting your practice from ego-driven desires and aligning your will with the highest good.

## Benefits of heart-centred breathing

- It calms the nervous system. Fear, stress, and tension soften.
- It brings attention into the body – from the mind and ego into our feeling.
- It opens our heart. We feel a closer connection to ourselves and others.
- It supports honest, caring communication – with more clarity and less fear.
- It helps us stay emotionally grounded. It's hard to harm others when we breathe with the heart.

The same breathing technique supports clear, compassionate communication. For its use in dialogue, see [[heart-centred-communication|heart-centred communication]].
