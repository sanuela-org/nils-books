# Cultivating Energetic Flow

## Definition

Cultivating energetic flow means keeping your vibration high through simple, everyday practices, so that [[magick|magick]] and healing come from a clear, energised place rather than from effort. The book [[kind-of-magick|A Kind of Magick]] describes it like this: before any ritual, raise your energy with the practices below — each one tunes your personal frequency, and the state they build together is the ground the [[nine-step-manifesting-ritual|nine-step manifesting ritual]] stands on.

## The high-vibration basics

### Intuitive hours

Rising with the sun and working in the early hours, before the day's noise begins, opens a natural window of stillness where intuitive perception is strongest. Use these hours for [[heart-centred-breathing|heart-centred breathing]], journaling or simply listening inwards.

### Brisk walking with the astral tingling

A brisk walk in nature moves energy through the body. As the circulation wakes, many people feel a light tingling in the skin — the astral body responding to the raised frequency. Instead of dismissing it, breathe into the tingling and let it spread: the walk becomes a moving meditation that charges the whole system.

### Humming

Humming vibrates the chest and the heart centre directly, settling the nervous system and lifting the mood within seconds. A few minutes of humming with awareness on the vibrations inside is one of the fastest ways to shift into a higher state.

### Silent handholding

Sitting in silence while holding hands — with a partner, a friend or a group — lets care and presence flow without a single word. It builds the felt sense of connection that underlies all companion work and every shared [[nine-step-manifesting-ritual|magickal ritual]]. A deeper, extended form of this energetic connection is the [[hugging-meditations|hugging meditation]], where the embrace opens a much stronger energetic exchange.
