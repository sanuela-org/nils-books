# Magickal Companions

## Definition

Magickal companions are the planets, saints, ascended masters, archangels, crystals and cards invited into ritual. Working with them is neither worship nor begging: it means respectfully aligning your intentions with a specific frequency of divine service. These beings are embodiments of divine qualities such as healing, protection, wisdom, compassion or justice. Calling on one is not asking an external deity to grant a wish. You align your own energy with their perfected vibration, like tuning an instrument to a specific note, and let their qualities flow through you. They act as mirrors, reflecting your soul's divine potential back to you; they do not give energy from outside, and worshiping them will not help you grow.

## Working with companions

The book [[kind-of-magick|A Kind of Magick]] describes it like this:

Invitation happens through heartfelt invocation, simply stating your wish for their supportive presence. You can invite the loving consciousness of a named being so that a named quality supports the highest collective good. Their presence often manifests as a felt sense of peace, a sudden clarity, or a warm, expansive energy in the heart. Your magickal companions gather at Step 3 of the [[nine-step-manifesting-ritual|nine-step manifesting ritual]], alongside [[archetypes|archetypes]] and [[the-five-elements|elements]].

As with any companion, ensure you not only take but also give. You can even ask your helpful partners what they wish for from you, and may receive an intuitive answer from your heart. Thank them for their support after working with them.

These beings act according to [[intention-and-divine-will|divine will]], always for the highest good of all. Their role is to empower, protect and illuminate your path, helping you become a clear vessel through which harmonious change can occur. The best approach is to come with an open heart and pure intentions: a personal communion and a union of consciousnesses dedicated to bringing more light, LOVE and healing into the world. Another system of divine companionship, the foundation archetypes, is covered on [[archetypes|Archetypes]].

## Planets and stars

A planet or star is more than a ball of rock or gas. Each is a superconscious being, an ancient, vast intelligence whose existence and purpose far surpass human understanding; through the lens of [[magick|magick]], the cosmos is a vast, interconnected family of highly developed beings. Our ancestors did not worship them out of superstition, but honoured them as powerful gods and goddesses who help to shape destiny and influence life on Earth. 

In shamanic and tribal traditions, the planets and stars are spirit guides, ancestors and living teachers, and the Sun is often a father figure or a chief deity. In Vedic astrology the planets are called 'Grahas', meaning 'influencers', acknowledging their conscious force in shaping human life. In the Greek and Roman pantheons, the planets were literally the gods: the fiery energy of Ares resonating in the red glow of Mars, the boundless LOVE and beauty of Aphrodite shimmering within Venus, the swift intelligence of Hermes animating Mercury. Working with planetary energies means entering a conscious relationship with these celestial deities, a loving collaboration between the microcosm and the macrocosm. Like saints, planets do not give energy from outside; they are mirrors and amplifiers of qualities already inside you.

### [[gaia|Gaia]] (Earth)

Core qualities: embodiment, nurturing, stability, grounding, fertility, cycles, belonging, sacred sensuality. 

Practical use: deepen the connection to the body and the natural world, ground spiritual insights into tangible reality, cultivate patience and resilience, manifest abundance and nurture projects to fruition, heal through sensory presence, strengthen community bonds and create safe spaces. 

Symbols: soil, mountains, seeds, caves, bones, clay, salt, Terra (Roman goddess). Shadow sides: stagnation, resistance to change, over-attachment to material security, possessiveness, inertia.

Affirmation: "I am deeply rooted, fully embodied, and nurtured by the abundant LOVE of the Earth."

What it teaches: how to be fully present in your body, to nurture and be nurtured, and to trust the slow, sure rhythms of growth. Growth path: walk barefoot on the earth, sit with your back against a tree, and ask where you need more stability and safety, and how you can better nurture yourself and others.

### Sun

Core qualities: consciousness, vitality, life force, self-expression, identity, purpose. 

Practical use: fuel visibility, charisma and leadership, strengthen health and stamina, empower creative expression, clarify purpose and long-term direction, build confidence to be seen, anchor joy into everyday life. 

Symbols: circle, candles, gold, sunlight, lion, fire, Sol (Roman god), Helios and Apollo (Greek gods). Shadow sides: ego inflation, arrogance, need for constant validation, dominating others, burnout from over-extending.

Affirmation: "I am confident, radiant, and aligned with my true purpose."

What it teaches: how to shine, be authentic and stand in your truth. Growth path: ask what your life at its brightest would look like, and what one step towards it you can take today.

### Mars

Core qualities: drive, will, courage, sexuality, action, boundaries. 

Practical use: fuel launches and new ventures, spark motivation and momentum, build resilience and discipline, strengthen boundaries and claim sovereignty, empower sexual vitality and passion projects, push through fear and initiate bold change. 

Symbols: red candles, iron, drumming, fire, cactus and thorns, pepper, physical movement, Ares (Greek god). Shadow sides: uncontrolled anger, aggression, conflict for its own sake, domination, impulsivity, destructive force, burnout from constant fighting.

Affirmation: "I act with courage and clarity. My will is strong, my boundaries are clear, and my energy is focused and powerful."

What it teaches: how to act decisively and claim your energy. Growth path: notice where you avoid conflict or action, and take a bold step, even a small one.

### Venus

Core qualities: LOVE, beauty, harmony, value, pleasure, attraction, abundance. 

Practical use: attract or deepen romantic relationships, increase wealth and the flow of resources, fuel artistic expression and aesthetics, cultivate self-LOVE and body confidence, strengthen friendships and community bonds, beautify environments. 

Symbols: flowers, perfumes, rose quartz, copper, apples, music, sweets, Aphrodite (Greek goddess). Shadow sides: vanity, over-indulgence, laziness, codependency, valuing appearance over substance, people-pleasing, using charm to manipulate.

Affirmation: "I am a magnet for LOVE and beauty. I attract harmonious relationships and abundant joy into my life, and I radiate appreciation for all that is good."

What it teaches: how to open to LOVE, pleasure and harmony. Growth path: ask where you can choose more pleasure; the more you value beauty and joy, the more you attract it.

### Jupiter

Core qualities: expansion, wisdom, faith, luck, benevolence, optimism, abundance. 

Practical use: grow business and career opportunities, expand travel and cross-cultural projects, fuel learning and spiritual growth, attract financial abundance, increase optimism and trust in life, empower community and philanthropic projects. 

Symbols: large candles, books, tin, oak trees, gratitude practices, purple and blue, Zeus (Greek god). Shadow sides: over-optimism, excess, overindulgence, laziness, exaggeration, dogmatism, self-righteousness.

Affirmation: "I am open to limitless abundance and wisdom. Opportunities expand for me, and I grow with joy and gratitude."

What it teaches: how to dream big and trust life. Growth path: step into opportunities that feel bigger than you.

### Saturn

Core qualities: structure, discipline, time, karma and consequence, responsibility, mastery, endurance. 

Practical use: build long-term structures, anchor discipline for health, study or spiritual practice, create clear boundaries, ensure financial stability and steady growth, commit to mastery of craft, strengthen resilience when facing challenges. 

Symbols: stones, bones, contracts, lead, black candles, Cronus (Greek god). Shadow sides: restriction, fear, rigidity, pessimism, control issues, harsh self-criticism, isolation, guilt, feeling burdened by responsibility.

Affirmation: "I build my life with patience and discipline. My structures are strong, my boundaries are clear, and my commitments are honored."

What it teaches: how to commit, endure and build lasting structures. Growth path: choose one area of life to master, and show up consistently.

### Uranus

Core qualities: freedom, innovation, awakening, breakthroughs, rebellion, the unexpected. 

Practical use: break free from limiting patterns or toxic ties, spark new inventions and artistic revolutions, empower movements for social change, awaken originality and radical self-expression, fuel sudden breakthroughs, shift personal identity into authenticity. 

Symbols: electricity, wind, fast rhythm, ecstatic movement. Shadow sides: rebelliousness for its own sake, unpredictability to the point of unreliability, detachment from human emotion, shocking others unnecessarily, chaotic disruption without a constructive vision.

Affirmation: "I embrace my unique truth and welcome positive change. I am free, awake, and open to inspired breakthroughs."

What it teaches: how to awaken, innovate and live authentically. Growth path: ask where you are stuck in old patterns, and dare to change something suddenly.

### Neptune

Core qualities: spirituality, dreams, imagination, compassion, mysticism, dissolution, transcendence, unity. 

Practical use: fuel creative projects in art, film, poetry and music, deepen meditation and mystical practice, heal through compassion and service, expand intuition and psychic gifts, dissolve limiting illusions, anchor collective projects based on unity. 

Symbols: water bowls, incense, oils, chanting, music, fog, mirrors, Poseidon (Greek god). Shadow sides: escapism through substances or fantasy, deception of self or others, confusion, lack of boundaries, victim mentality, delusion, being lost in the "fog".

Affirmation: "I trust the flow of life and listen to my soul's dreams. I am connected to infinite LOVE and creative inspiration."

What it teaches: how to dream, surrender and connect to spirit. Growth path: meditate, create or serve others.

### Pluto

Core qualities: transformation, power, shadow, death and rebirth, the unconscious, regeneration. 

Practical use: release addictions, toxic ties and limiting beliefs, reclaim hidden power and authority, fuel shadow work and trauma integration, support personal rebirth and identity shifts, create legacies that endure beyond one life, empower collective transformation. 

Symbols: obsidian, black candles, burial and seed rituals, fire, phoenix, volcanoes, deep caves, Hades (Greek god), Shiva (the Hindu god of destruction and regeneration). Shadow sides: obsession, control, manipulation, power struggles, destructive rage, paranoia, forcing transformation on others, dwelling in the "underworld" without seeking rebirth.

Affirmation: "I release the past with courage and embrace my true power. I am constantly reborn, stronger and more authentic with every cycle."

What it teaches: how to face your shadows and reclaim power. Growth path: ask what part of you must die for your true self to live.

## The Moon as a cosmic mirror

Across traditions the Moon is seen as a great reflector. It serves as a cosmic mirror for consciousness, reflecting hidden, subconscious aspects back to us and making them visible. Its cycles of waxing and waning mirror the rhythms of our own lives: beginnings, growth, fulfilment, release, rest.

Magickal practice can be synchronised with the phases. The New Moon offers a fresh start, an ideal time for planting seeds in the fertile darkness of your own potential, starting new projects, setting clear intentions and initiating cycles of growth. The Full Moon radiates peak reflected energy, illuminating what has been growing within us: an ideal time for charging, acknowledging and revealing truth, practising gratitude, and releasing what is complete. The Moon reminds us that outer reality reflects inner state; by observing its dance we learn to observe the cycles of our own soul.

## The power of timing

Magick can be a dance of conscious timing. The seven-day week was created to help remember and connect with the distinct frequencies of each planetary consciousness, a rhythmic tool that can powerfully focus and amplify intentions. Yet your focused intention is always the strongest driving force: if you feel called to work with Venus's energy on a Saturday, your conscious decision takes precedence over the calendar. The correspondences:

- Monday: Moon (subconscious, dreams, emotions)
- Tuesday: Mars (courage, action, conflict)
- Wednesday: Mercury (communication, intellect, travel)
- Thursday: Jupiter (expansion, abundance, wisdom)
- Friday: Venus (LOVE, beauty, harmony)
- Saturday: Saturn (structure, discipline, release)
- Sunday: Sun (vitality, identity, success)

## Ascended masters, archangels and saints

Ascended masters are beings who achieved Christ-consciousness, often through human experience, and now guide others on the path. Archangels are celestial messengers of immense power and light who oversee vast cosmic functions. Saints are souls recognised for their wholeness, exceptional virtue and service, often specialising in specific areas of life.

### Ascended masters

- **Jesus Christ, the Embodiment of Unconditional Love.** His energy is one of profound compassion, forgiveness and sacrificial LOVE. He teaches the power of the heart, resurrection as transformation, and the Light of Christ within every being.
- **Mary Magdalene, the Alchemist of Love.** She embodies redeemed feminine power, teaching that deep, heartfelt emotions and sacred sensuality are direct pathways to the divine. She transforms wounds of shame into unshakable spiritual authority, and celebrates the power of feminine Christ consciousness.
- **Mother Mary, the Divine Mother.** She embodies pure, nurturing, unconditional maternal LOVE, comfort and grace. She offers solace in times of grief, protection for children, and healing for the wounds of the heart with gentle compassion.
- **Paramhansa Yogananda, the Bridge Between Worlds.** The author of *Autobiography of a Yogi*, who wove the ancient wisdom of the East into the scientific framework of the West. Call upon him for support with self-realisation and meditation, or to better understand the cosmic laws that govern our reality.
- **Mahavatar Babaji, the Immortal Yogi-Christ.** He represents the pinnacle of spiritual mastery, divine grace and the promise of liberation in this lifetime. His presence signifies a direct download of transformative energy for those ready to accelerate their spiritual evolution.
- **Buddha, Siddhartha Gautama, the Awakened One.** He embodies perfect enlightenment, compassion and peace beyond all suffering. He is the master of the Middle Way, teaching the liberation that comes from mindful presence, detachment and the cessation of desire. Call upon his energy for profound inner peace, clarity of mind, and to see the true nature of reality.
- **Krishna, the Divine Guide and Lover.** The embodiment of divine joy, LOVE and playful wisdom. His energy teaches righteous action performed with LOVE and without attachment to its fruits. Invite him for guidance through life's moral dilemmas and to infuse your path with bliss and devotion.
- **Saint Germain, the Alchemist.** The master of the violet flame of transmutation, which transforms lower vibrational energy such as fear, karma and limitation into light and freedom. He supports alchemical change, spiritual freedom and manifestation.

### Archangels

- **Archangel Michael, the Protector.** He carries a sword of blue flame that cuts through fear and illusion, freeing you from psychic bonds and negative influences. Call upon him for courage, protection, and the strength to set clear, fiery boundaries.
- **Archangel Raphael, the Healer.** His emerald green energy brings profound physical, emotional and spiritual healing. He guides healers, supports those in caring professions, and brings comfort and restoration to all in need.
- **Archangel Gabriel, the Messenger.** The divine trumpeter who brings clarity and announcements of new beginnings. Gabriel supports writers, teachers and messengers, helping to communicate truth with compassion and strength.
- **Archangel Uriel, the Illuminator.** He holds a lantern that lights the way through darkness and confusion, offering wisdom, creative solutions and divine insight during challenging times.
- **Archangel Chamuel, the Beacon of Compassionate Love.** He helps you find what is sought, whether a lost item, a new relationship, inner peace, or the LOVE within your own heart. His pink light mends disagreements and strengthens connections through understanding.
- **Archangel Jophiel, the Patron of Beauty and Illumination.** She helps beautify thoughts and environments, transforming clutter into order and negativity into light. Invite her to open your eyes to the beauty around you and to bring inspiration to creative projects.
- **Archangel Zadkiel, the Angel of Benevolence and Memory.** He holds the violet light of mercy and forgiveness, assisting in transmuting painful memories and releasing resentment. Call upon him to foster a compassionate perspective and to enhance spiritual study and retention.
- **Archangel Haniel, the Embodiment of Lunar Grace.** She connects you to the rhythms and intuition of the moon cycles, enhancing clairvoyance and spiritual grace. She brings elegance and harmony to emotional experiences and relationships.

### Saints

- **Saint Francis of Assisi, the Patron of Animals and Nature.** He embodies a heartfelt kinship with all living beings, simplicity, joyful service, and deep reverence for Gaia and all her beings.
- **Saint Therese of Lisieux, the Little Flower, the Patron of Small Things.** She teaches that small, everyday acts performed with great LOVE are the highest form of sanctity. She helps find the sacred in the mundane and brings humility and sweetness to your path.
- **Saint Jude, the Patron of Hopeless Causes.** The companion for when situations seem impossible. He brings hope, intervention and miraculous solutions when all other doors appear closed, supporting unwavering faith in desperate times.
- **Saint Anthony, the Finder of Lost Things.** Beyond locating physical objects, he helps recover lost hope, lost joy, lost passion and lost connection to the divine, restoring what has been forgotten or misplaced on your path.
- **Saint Christopher, the Patron of Travelers and Safe Passage.** Traditionally invoked for physical journeys, his energy also protects and guides you through any form of transition or life change, ensuring safe passage through challenging terrain.
- **Saint Dymphna, the Patron of Mental and Emotional Well-being.** She offers solace, peace and comfort to those suffering from anxiety, stress, mental turmoil and emotional trauma. She is a beacon of calm and stability for the mind and nerves.
- **Saint Joseph, the Patron of Fathers, Workers and Home.** The embodiment of steadfastness, diligence and silent strength. He assists in providing for a family, finding employment and protecting the home, bringing practical, structural stability to any project or endeavor.

## Crystals

Crystals and stones are companions in ritual, each holding a unique frequency and energetic signature forged deep within Gaia. They are not used for their supposed properties, but invited to partner with you, amplifying and anchoring intentions with their stable, crystalline energy. The precise structure of a crystal holds a vibrational pattern with exceptional clarity: a rose quartz on the ritual tray becomes a concentrated source of heart-centred LOVE vibrations, not just a symbol. Crystals stabilise and ground energies; during powerful rituals they help contain the raised energies, preventing dissipation and creating a potent, cohesive field.

Choosing one is an intuitive process. Traditional correspondences can guide, but trust your intuition and choose the stone that feels right for your current needs. You may feel drawn to the right stone, or hold different stones and notice which resonates, creating a subtle pull or a sense of calm. Honour the crystals as the living, conscious partners they are. Widely available stones that are powerful helpers for any practice:

- Clear Quartz: amplification, clarity, focus, energy channeling, spiritual awakening
- Snow Quartz: gentle purity, innocence, softening emotions, tranquility, heartfelt truth
- Smoky Quartz: grounding, purification, releasing negativity, emotional detox, transformation

- Amethyst: intuition, spiritual protection, peace, stress relief, connection to higher consciousness
- Citrine: abundance, manifestation, personal power, joy, creativity
- Black Tourmaline: protection, grounding, negativity shielding, electromagnetic cleansing
- Selenite: purification, mental clarity, connecting to higher guidance, cleansing other crystals
- Amazonite: truth, harmony, clear communication, soothing energy, emotional balance
- Carnelian: motivation, courage, vitality, creativity, confidence
- Labradorite: transformation, magic, psychic protection, awakening inner power, intuition
- Moonstone: intuition, divine feminine, new beginnings, emotional balance, grace
- Hematite: grounding, stability, focus, strength, balancing the body
- Green Aventurine: luck, opportunity, prosperity, heart healing, emotional calm

- Red Jasper: nurturing stability, vitality, endurance, connection to Earth

## Tarot and oracle cards

Tarot and oracle cards are typically associated with fortune-telling, but in magickal rituals they facilitate open dialogue and deepen the work already underway, offering additional elements. They primarily introduce a new perspective, unexpected energy or clarifying insight the conscious mind might have overlooked, opening a channel for intuitive guidance from the deeper self and spirit companions.

The card's imagery provides a focal point that enriches the work. An empress figure could inspire embracing nurturing and creative abundance; a wolf might encourage trusting your instincts and the strength of your community; a turbulent sea could indicate the need to work with [[the-five-elements|the five elements]], processing emotions through Water; a bright sun could encourage invoking clarity and confidence. The cards act as a bridge, translating subtle guidance from your spirit team into a tangible form.

Cards are particularly valuable when you feel stuck or want to break free from habitual thinking. The 'random' draw brings the card you need rather than the one you want, disrupting the ego's narrative with an element of divine surprise. The method consciously invites [[synchronicity|synchronicity]] into the ritual structure, affirming that you are not working alone: your [[personal-universe|personal universe]] is intelligent and communicative, always ready to provide guidance, support and new insights.
