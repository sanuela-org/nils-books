# Sanuela Retreats

## Definition

A retreat at Finca Sanuela in Tenerife brings the themes of the books into lived experience: a few days or weeks shaped entirely around your current needs and readiness, with no fixed programme and no pressure to be anywhere other than exactly where you are. Whether you come alone or with a partner, the setting is safe and unhurried — a place where new ideas about your very own life stop being theory and become practice.

## What you can practise and explore

- Heart-centred breathing and speaking — the foundation of [[heart-centred-communication|honest, loving communication]]
- Heart-sharing circles — the daily heart-share as a real, lived experience ([[heart-centred-communication#Practice: Daily heart-share|complete practice]])
- [[allowances-and-boundaries|Allowances and boundaries]] conversations — guided exercises to find your own approach and express it
- Identifying and working with [[growing-with-triggers|triggers]], walking through [[meeting-fear|fears]], the [[jealousy-as-a-team-process|team approach to jealousy]], practising [[non-attachment-and-compersion|compersion]]
- [[shadow-work|Inner shadow work]] — meeting the old wounds and patterns that drive reactions, guided and held safely
- [[deep-surrender#Practice: Forgiveness|Forgiveness]] and gratitude work — releasing what has been holding you back
- Somatic practices — hand-holding, [[hugging-meditations|hugging meditations]] and other practices that build the body's capacity for trust and openness
- Optional fasting or raw food cleansing — quieting the energetic noise for deeper inner receptivity
- Individual visioning — what does my future life actually look like? Who is in it? What do I need to feel safe in it?
- Individual sessions — one-to-one time with Nils to look at your specific situation

## What makes this different

- It is immersive and somatic: a few days where the practices are experienced in the body and become part of daily life.
- It is personalised, private and small: the one-to-one retreat is shaped around where you actually are.
- It is a real-life example: Finca Sanuela itself, and Nils's way of being, is a living embodiment of everything the books describe.

## The forms a retreat can take

- **Deep Presence & Heart Connection** — a solo full-immersion retreat for a fresh start: after a breakup, years of emotional numbness or quiet loneliness. Honest sharing, inner work and forgiveness help release old protective layers, guided personally by Nils.
- **Ready to Love Again** — a private healing retreat for singles: recognising old relationship patterns, clarifying values and non-negotiables, and building a vision of the partnership you are ready for. Guided shadow work and somatic closeness practice are woven through the days, and a photo session in the Tenerife landscape captures who you have become.
- **Heart & Soul Companion Training** — learning to offer others conscious, caring, non-sexual touch: hand-holding, hugging meditations and energetic heart work, grounded in safety and consent and yin and yang balance. Your own inner opening is part of the training.
- **Network of LOVE** as a lived experience, alone or as a couple: honest communication, working with jealousy, compersion, conscious touch and individual visioning, shaped entirely around where you are.
- **The Darkroom Experience** — several days and nights in complete darkness in a quiet darkroom space at the finca, with daily integration sessions. There is space for deep inner processes, visions and rest.

## Possible lengths

- 3–6 days: a first guided experience
- 7–14 days: deeper immersion, more space for individual work and somatic practices.
- More than two weeks: time for deeper connection, shared daily life and whatever wants to emerge.
